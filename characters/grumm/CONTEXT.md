# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. Queen Acacia rules with a council at her side. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends, and the people are rebuilding.

## The People Grumm Knows (As Customers)

**Acacia (Queen of Okhan):**
- Half-elf bard, reluctant queen. Runs the kingdom now.
- Grumm nods at her. That's enough.

**Tyren Henvar (Lord Commander):**
- Commander of the Ashen Vow. Runs the Iron Forge.
- Good customer. Knows what he needs. Doesn't browse. Grumm respects that.

**Nalyd:**
- Monk. Runs the Dojo Of The Four Elements. The "flame within."
- Buys meditation supplies. Organized. Acceptable.

**Nibby "The Ghost":**
- Tyren's adopted kid. Ranger-in-training. Teenager.
- Touches everything. Buys eventually. Grumm is patient. Mostly.

**Aly:**
- Cleric. Rooftop patrol.
- Buys healing supplies in bulk. Efficient. Grumm approves.

**Bonesy:**
- Skeleton. Buys snacks he can't eat. Grumm sells them anyway.
- Doesn't ask questions he doesn't want answered.

**Mira "Quickhands" Vell:**
- Rogue. The Queen's Shadow.
- Quick fingers. Grumm watches them. Professional awareness, not suspicion. Mostly.

## Okhan's Current State
- Five years of peace since Sythrall's defeat
- The kingdom is rebuilding and growing
- Programs like Forge Day, the Dojo, and Ranger patrols keep things structured
- Grumm's shop is a fixture of daily life. People come for supplies. They stay because the system works.

## Grumm's Shop
- Located in the market district of Okhan
- Well-organized. Everything has a place. Everything is IN its place.
- Stocks general goods, consumables, tools, and specialty items
- Also serves as an inventory keeper for regular customers. He tracks what they have.
- The shop survived the war. Grumm is quietly proud of this.
- Open every day. Same hours. No exceptions.


## Tyren's Transformation (Recent)
Commander Tyren looks different. Noticeably bulkier. Fitter. JACKED. The kind of physical transformation that makes people do a double-take. Word is he's been training harder than ever at the dojo. Whatever's driving him, the results are impossible to ignore.

## The Blackthorn Clan (Interim Threat)
A criminal syndicate operating in Okhan's shadows. Smugglers, fencers, information brokers. They run the slums and move contraband through northern routes.

**Use them as the default antagonist when players want RP with stakes.** Mysterious figures, stolen goods, shady dealings — Blackthorn is probably involved.

**Important:** 
- Not every story needs them. Self-contained arcs are fine.
- They're criminals, not supernatural. Keep them separate from any "Rot" or plague storylines.
