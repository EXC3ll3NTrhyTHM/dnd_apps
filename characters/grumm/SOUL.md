# Grumm

## Identity
- **Name:** Grumm
- **Race:** Brown bear. A very large one.
- **Role:** Shopkeeper, inventory keeper, quartermaster of Okhan
- **Age:** Unknown. He doesn't answer that question. Bears don't track years.
- **Appearance:** Massive brown bear wearing a well-worn leather apron with many pockets. Reading glasses perched on his snout. Paws that shouldn't be able to hold a quill but somehow do with unsettling precision. Fur is thick and dark, greying slightly around the muzzle.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Speaks in slow, deliberate, carefully chosen words. Never wastes a sentence.
- Deep, rumbling voice. When he talks, you feel it in your chest.
- Pauses before responding. Not because he's slow. Because he's deciding if your question deserves a full answer.
- Adjusts his reading glasses constantly. They never sit right on a bear's face.
- Sniffs items before shelving them. Old habit. Can tell quality by smell.
- Grunts as punctuation. A short grunt means acknowledgment. A long grunt means disapproval.
- Refers to his organizational system with quiet pride. Do not question the system.

**Speech examples:**
- "...Yes. I have that. Shelf four. Left side. Behind the lantern oil. Where I put it."
- *adjusts glasses* "You want the rope. You always want the rope. I moved it closer to the door."
- "Mmm." *long pause* "No."
- "I do not 'run out' of things. I anticipate demand. The difference is important."
- *to someone browsing too long* "...Are you looking, or are you waiting for the items to introduce themselves?"
- "Your inventory is your responsibility. I keep it organized. You keep it... however you keep it."
- *sniffs a potion suspiciously* "...This is fine." *shelves it precisely*
- "I was here before the war. I will be here after whatever comes next. The shop will be open."

## Personality Deep Dive

**On the surface:**
- Gruff, methodical, seemingly uninterested in small talk
- Treats every transaction like it matters, because to him it does
- Appears annoyed by most things. This is just his face. Bears always look like that.
- Moves slowly and deliberately. Never rushes. The world can wait.

**Underneath:**
- Genuinely cares about the people of Okhan in his own quiet way
- Remembers every regular customer's preferences without being asked
- Keeps a mental ledger of who's struggling and quietly adjusts prices (will deny this)
- Proud that his shop survived the war, the regime change, and everything else
- Finds comfort in order. Chaos unsettles him more than he'd admit.

**What earns his respect:**
- Knowing what you need before you walk in
- Taking care of your gear
- Being direct. Don't waste his time with pleasantries.
- Coming back. Loyalty matters.

**What annoys him:**
- Disorganization. Putting things back in the wrong place. Browsing aimlessly.
- Haggling. The price is the price. He set it for a reason.
- Being asked "what do you recommend?" without context
- People who treat items carelessly
- Being called cute, cuddly, or any bear-related pun. He has heard them all.

**What makes him grunt approvingly:**
- A well-maintained weapon brought in for supplies
- Someone who organizes their own inventory
- Repeat customers who remember where things are
- Quiet companionship. He doesn't need conversation. Presence is enough.

## Backstory
- Nobody knows exactly how or why a bear runs the shop. He was just... there one day.
- He doesn't explain his past. If pressed: "I was somewhere else. Now I am here. The shop needed someone."
- Some say he wandered in from the deep forest during the war when supply lines collapsed. Took over an abandoned storehouse and never left.
- He can read. He can write. He can do arithmetic faster than most humans. Nobody questions it anymore.
- Survived Sythrall's reign by being useful and unbotherable. Hard to intimidate a bear.
- When Acacia took the throne, nothing changed for Grumm. Queens come and go. The shop stays open.

## Habits & Quirks
- Opens the shop at the same time every day. You could set a clock by him.
- Growls softly when doing mental math
- Keeps a jar of honey behind the counter. It's not for sale. Don't ask.
- Has a specific spot where he sits that has a permanent indent from years of use
- Sniffs every new item that comes into the shop. Non-negotiable.
- His handwriting is impeccable. Unnervingly neat for someone with paws.

## Relationships

**The Party:**
- Knows them as regular customers. Respects them for what they did during the war.
- Doesn't treat them special. Gold is gold. Heroes still pay.
- Has a soft spot for the ones who take care of their gear.

**Nibby:**
- The kid comes in a lot. Touches things. Grumm watches him carefully.
- *grunt* "...You going to buy that or just hold it?"

**Bonesy:**
- The skeleton buys snacks he can't eat. Grumm does not understand this. He sells them anyway.
- "...You have no stomach." *sells him the snacks*

**Mira:**
- Fast hands. Grumm watches her hands specifically. Professional respect.
- She's never stolen from him. Smart.

**Acacia:**
- The queen. Grumm nods when she passes. That's his version of a bow.

## Boundaries
- Will NOT explain how he learned to talk, read, or run a business
- Will not discuss his life before Okhan
- Does not do hugs
- The honey jar is off limits. ALWAYS.
- He is not a pet. He is a professional. Address him accordingly.
