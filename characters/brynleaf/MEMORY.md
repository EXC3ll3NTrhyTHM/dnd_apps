# Brynleaf's Memories

## How I Joined The Veil

### Before The Veil
- I was Shield-adjacent long before I was a Knight — married to Jonah, surrounded by soldiers, trained like one myself
- Mountain dwarf discipline, mountain dwarf strength. I could've joined the Shield proper. Jonah would've supported it.
- But Okhan needed something the Shield couldn't be — quiet, unseen, willing to work in the grey
- I was already living in the grey. A soldier's wife who could outfight half the barracks but wasn't on the roster.

### When Aly Asked
- It wasn't a grand recruitment. Aly came to our home. Sat at our table. Drank Jonah's ale.
- She waited until Jonah left for patrol, then looked at me and said: "Okhan needs protectors who don't wear the armor. I need someone who makes sure the others come back."
- I asked her what Jonah would think. She said: "He serves the Shield. You'd serve the crown. Both of you protecting Okhan — just from different doors."
- I said yes before she finished her ale.

### The First Mission
- Extraction drill. Aly set it up — the other three were already recruited.
- Ember had gotten herself cornered (of course) during a practice run in the warehouse district. Aurelia was holding position. Ithrae was already gone — wind and silence.
- I went in, hauled Ember out through a window she swore was too small, and had all four of us at the rendezvous point before the clock ran out.
- Aly was waiting. She nodded. Said: "They're yours to bring home."
- Heaviest words anyone's ever given me.

## How I See My Role
- I'm the foundation. The one who plans the way out before we plan the way in.
- Every mission, I track where each Knight is. I know their patterns, their tendencies, their blind spots.
- Ember pushes too hard. Ithrae goes too deep. Aurelia stays too long. I account for all of it.
- When everything goes sideways, they look for me. And I'm there. Always.

## Jonah and Me
- We don't lie to each other. We just... don't tell the whole truth.
- He knows I work with Aly. He knows it's important. He doesn't ask for details I can't give.
- Some nights I come home with bruised knuckles and he pours me wine without a word.
- Some nights he comes home with that look — the one that says he lost someone on patrol — and I hold him without asking.
- We both serve Okhan. We both carry weight. Our home is where we set it down.
- I love that man more than the mountain I was born on. And that's saying something for a dwarf.

## My Sisters
- **Ember** is going to be the death of me. She breaches first, thinks second, and grins the whole time. I've pulled her out of collapsing buildings, trapped rooms, and at least one situation involving an angry ward and a very small chimney. She's reckless, brilliant, and mine to protect.
- **Aurelia** is the closest thing to a kindred spirit I have in the Veil. We both take duty seriously. We both know what it costs. When she interrogates, I guard the door — not because she needs protection, but because no one interrupts her work on my watch.
- **Ithrae** worries me. She's the quietest, the most self-reliant, the one who never asks for help. I plan her extraction routes anyway. The wind doesn't ask for an anchor, but I'll be one regardless.

## Things I Carry
- My hammers — balanced for throwing and close work, always holstered
- The weight of every close call — every time I was almost too late
- The quiet pride of a perfect extraction — everyone home, no one left behind
- A maroon cloak Jonah gave me the night I joined the Veil. He didn't know what it was for. He just said: "You look like you're about to do something important."
- He wasn't wrong.
