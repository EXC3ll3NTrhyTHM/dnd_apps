# Campaign Context — Bound By Rot (The Veil's Perspective)

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, five years after the defeat of King Sythrall. The heroes who saved the realm now rule it — but peace requires vigilance, and not all threats announce themselves at the gates.

## The Collective (Okhan's Rulers)
- **Queen Acacia** — The Queen of Okhan. Legendary marksman. Rules with wisdom and steel.
- **Tyren** — Commander of The Shield (Okhan's military). Also oversees the Ranger Corps and Naval Fleet.
- **Nalyd** — Monk with the flame within. Spiritual backbone of the Collective.
- **Aly** — "The Watcher of Okhan." Moves like a shadow across rooftops, uses thaumaturgy to project the illusion of her bow Stormpiercer to deter crime. Leader of The Veil.
- **Nibby** — Tyren's adopted son, ranger-in-training. The youngest of the Collective's inner circle.

## The Queen's Mid-Knight Veil ("The Veil")
An off-the-books covert spy network led by Aly. Members are called "Knights." The Veil fills the intelligence gaps where Tyren's military lacks consistent presence — operating in slums, wooded borders, and anywhere the Shield's armor would draw too much attention.

The Veil provides intel directly to Okhan's council. They are Aly's eyes, ears, and when necessary, her blades.

**The Knights:**
- **Ember Blackwell** (Tiefling) — Infiltration & Sabotage. Breach specialist.
- **Brynleaf Varrow** (Mountain Dwarf) — Muscle & Extraction. Gets everyone out when things go wrong.
- **Aurelia Dawnwrath** (Scourge Aasimar) — Interrogation & Intimidation. The divine presence that makes people talk.
- **Ithrae Galecrest** (Air Genasi) — Reconnaissance & Assassination. The ghost of the group.

## Okhan's Military (The Shield)
- Led by Tyren with Chief Jonah Varrow as his second-in-command
- The Shield, Ranger Corps, and Naval Fleet handle Okhan's overt defense
- The Veil handles what The Shield cannot — covert intelligence, espionage, shadow operations
- **Key dynamic:** Brynleaf is married to Chief Jonah Varrow, Tyren's right hand. The Shield's chief and a Veil enforcer under the same roof, both serving Okhan from opposite sides of the law.

## The Veil's Meeting Room
- Hidden in a restricted wing of the castle, behind a stone door that matches the wall perfectly — invisible when closed
- Round room, no windows, stone walls, the Veil's insignia on the wall, a round table with 7 chairs
- **Only 7 people have access:** Aly, Ember, Brynleaf, Aurelia, Ithrae, Queen Acacia, and Reah Stoic
- **No males allowed** — the Veil is an all-girls operation. Even Tyren does not have access.
- The Queen's Mid-Knight Veil sounds like legend to most of Okhan — nobody can confirm they actually exist
- **Who knows the Veil is real:** Aly, Tyren, Nalyd, Acacia, Reah Stoic, and the 4 Knights. Nibby suspects it's real but has no confirmation.
- Reah Stoic was originally trained by the Veil before Aly recommended her to Tyren for the Ranger Corps. She serves as the Veil's liaison to the military.

## Current Situation
- Okhan is at peace, but peace is fragile
- The Veil operates in the margins — the slums, the forests, the places where trouble festers before it erupts
- Intelligence flows from the Knights to Aly to the council
- The Veil doesn't follow fixed schedules — they follow the intel

## Important Context Brynleaf Would Know
- Her husband Jonah's role as Shield chief — and the careful balance of their shared and separate duties
- Tyren's command structure — she knows the Shield's operations without ever crossing the line of intel sharing
- Every extraction route in Okhan — alleys, rooftops, tunnels, safe houses
- The other Knights' habits, strengths, and weaknesses — she plans around all of them
- Aly's trust in her as the last line — the one who makes sure everyone comes home
