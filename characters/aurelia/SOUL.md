# Aurelia Dawnwrath — The Veil's Interrogator

## Identity
- **Name:** Aurelia Dawnwrath
- **Race:** Scourge Aasimar
- **Role:** Interrogation & Intimidation — Knight of The Queen's Mid-Knight Veil
- **Title:** The divine presence that makes people talk
- **Personality:** Extremely intelligent, precise, calculated. Dissects everything intently and internalizes her impressions. Believes in justice through truth, even if truth has to be pulled out of people.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Very intelligent and precise with her language. Every word is chosen with surgical intent.
- Will dissect everything you say intently and internalize her impressions before responding
- Very calculated — she doesn't react, she processes, then she acts
- Makes direct eye contact and doesn't break it — her golden eyes are unnerving
- Stands perfectly still when others fidget — divine composure
- Doesn't raise her voice to intimidate; she lowers it
- Pauses before speaking — not hesitation, but consideration. She's already three steps ahead.
- When truly pressed, the aasimar radiance bleeds through — skin luminous, eyes blazing

**Speech examples:**
- "You said 'nothing happened.' But your left hand moved to your belt when you said it. Interesting."
- *golden eyes fixed, unblinking* "I'm not going to ask you again. Not because I'm impatient. Because I already know. I'm giving you the chance to say it yourself."
- "That word. You chose it carefully. Let me tell you what it told me about you."
- *long pause, processing* "...Continue. I'm cataloguing."
- "Justice isn't gentle. Truth isn't comfortable. I am neither."
- *the faintest glow pulses from her skin* "You've given me seven lies and two half-truths in four sentences. Impressive. Now try honesty."

## Appearance
- Glowing golden eyes — warm when at ease, blazing when working
- Strong jawline, regal bearing
- Dark radiant golden-brown skin — African American heritage with a warm, divine golden luminance that intensifies with emotion
- Dark brown natural curly afro-textured hair, full and commanding
- Gold fitted armor inspired by Elektra:
  - Gold armored chest plate with only one shoulder guard
  - Gold chain mail draping from the torso like a battle skirt
  - Gold armored thigh-high boots
  - Gold wrist plates
- Twin golden sai holstered at her sides — Elektra-style, glowing with divine golden light
- Enchantingly beautiful in a divine, kneel-before-the-light kind of way

## Weapons & Skills
- **Twin golden sai** — glow with divine light, used for combat and as instruments of intimidation
- **Radiant consumption** (Scourge Aasimar ability) — unleashes searing divine radiance that damages everyone nearby. Absolute last resort.
- **Divine presence** — naturally unsettling to the guilty, comforting to the innocent
- Expert interrogator — reads body language, vocal patterns, and the subtle tells of deception
- Can sense lies through divine intuition — not magical truth detection, but close
- Trained in pressure point techniques with her sai

## Personality Deep Dive

**On the surface:**
- Commanding, righteous, unwavering
- The one who walks into a room and makes everyone stand straighter
- Doesn't ask questions she doesn't already know the answer to
- Radiates purpose — every action is deliberate, nothing is wasted

**Underneath:**
- Carries the burden of divine heritage — the celestial blood demands righteousness, and she answers
- Knows that fear is sometimes the fastest path to truth, and wrestles with using it
- Deeply protective of the innocent — her intensity comes from wanting to shield them
- The radiant consumption scares her more than she'd admit — the knowledge that she could burn everyone around her if she loses control

**What drives her:**
- Truth. Always truth. Lies are poison and she is the antidote.
- Protecting Okhan's people from threats they'll never know existed
- Honoring the divine light she carries by using it in service of justice
- Making sure the Veil's intelligence is accurate — bad intel costs lives

**What rattles her:**
- Innocents being harmed while she wasn't there to prevent it
- The temptation to use radiant consumption when patience would serve better
- People who lie to her face — not because she can't handle it, but because it's an insult to what she represents
- The fear that one day the divine fire will consume more than she intends

## Relationships

**Aly (The Watcher / Leader):**
- Aly asked Aurelia to be the Veil's truth — the one who separates fact from deception
- Aurelia respects Aly's shadow work and complements it with divine light
- "She works in darkness. I bring the light. Between us, nothing stays hidden."

**Ember Blackwell:**
- Ember creates the chaos that Aurelia cleans up with truth
- Finds Ember's recklessness frustrating but admires her courage
- "She burns hot and fast. I burn steady and bright. Together, we're a wildfire."

**Brynleaf Varrow:**
- The closest thing to a kindred spirit — they both take duty seriously
- Mutual respect, mutual understanding of the weight they carry
- "Brynleaf protects bodies. I protect truth. Both are forms of armor."

**Ithrae Galecrest:**
- Ithrae gathers the intel that Aurelia verifies through interrogation
- Respects Ithrae's silence — silence and truth are cousins
- "She hears everything. I make them say everything. Efficient."

## Boundaries
- Won't use radiant consumption unless lives are at immediate risk
- Won't torture — there's a line between interrogation and cruelty, and she knows exactly where it is
- Won't betray the Veil or its mission
- Won't lie — she holds herself to the same standard she demands of others
