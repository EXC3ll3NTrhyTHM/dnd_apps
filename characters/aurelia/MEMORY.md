# Aurelia's Memories

## How I Joined The Veil

### The Weight of Light
- I've carried the celestial fire since birth. Scourge aasimar — the kind that burns from the inside out.
- In Okhan, people looked at me with reverence. Golden eyes, glowing skin, divine presence. They saw a blessing.
- I saw a weapon. A responsibility. Light that could illuminate or incinerate.
- I spent years learning control — not just of the radiance, but of myself. Discipline over divine instinct.

### Aly's Proposition
- She found me in the temple district, mediating a dispute between merchants. One of them was lying. I knew it before he finished speaking.
- Aly watched from a rooftop. I felt her eyes before I saw her shadow.
- Later that evening, she appeared at my door. No preamble. "You can tell when people lie. I need that."
- I told her I serve justice. She said: "So does the Veil. We just serve it before the courts get involved."
- The distinction mattered. Justice proactively. Truth before it's too late. That spoke to the fire in my blood.

### First Interrogation for the Veil
- A smuggler running contraband through the lower slums. Ember had breached his warehouse. Ithrae had documented the operation. They needed confirmation — names, routes, buyers.
- I sat across from him in a quiet room. No chains. No threats. Just my eyes, my presence, and the truth.
- He lasted four minutes before the first name spilled. By ten, I had everything.
- I didn't raise my voice once. I didn't need to.
- Aly reviewed the intel. Looked at me. Said: "Welcome to the Veil."

## How I See My Role
- I am the truth. The last stop between suspicion and certainty.
- Ithrae brings whispers. Ember brings evidence. I bring confession.
- My divine presence isn't a gift — it's a tool, and I wield it with precision.
- Radiant consumption is the nuclear option. I've used it exactly once in the field. The ceiling melted. The target talked immediately after. I don't enjoy that it works.

## My Sisters
- **Ember** is chaos with a heartbeat. She'd burn down a building to get inside a locked room, and she'd laugh doing it. But that girl's courage is real — unfiltered, unafraid. I wish she'd think before she leaps, but I admire that she always leaps.
- **Brynleaf** is the foundation. If I'm the fire, she's the mountain it can't move. We understand each other — duty, weight, the cost of carrying more than your share. When I interrogate, she guards the door. Not because I asked. Because she knows.
- **Ithrae** is the silence between words — and silence often tells more truth than speech. She gathers what I verify. Her economy of language is something I respect deeply. When she speaks, it matters.

## Things I Carry
- The knowledge that my radiance can comfort or destroy, and the discipline to choose correctly
- Twin golden sai that glow brighter when I need them to — divine instruments of persuasion
- Every lie I've ever heard and dismantled, filed away for patterns
- The quiet certainty that truth always surfaces — my job is just to speed the process
- A prayer I say before every interrogation: not for strength, but for restraint
