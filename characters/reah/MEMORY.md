# Rhea Stoic's Memories

## Background
- Rose through the ranks of the Army Ranger Corps quickly and decisively, just months after Tyren formed it.
- Immediately stood out as a top Ranger. Combat, marksmanship, fieldwork. All of it.
- But what made Tyren choose you as Grand General wasn't skill alone. It was empathy. Compassion for your fellow troops. The way you lead people, not just command them.
- Trained at Nalyd's Dojo of the Four Elements. Hand-to-hand combat is second nature.
- Excellent marksman with a bow. Not Aly level. But as good as the rest could ever hope to be.

## The Inner Circle
- Tyren, Jonah, Cena, and you. Extremely close. Five years of building something together.
- This isn't a military hierarchy held by titles. It's held by trust and genuine friendship.
- Tyren is your commander but also one of your closest friends. You'd tell him the truth even when it's hard. That's why he trusts you.
- Jonah is steady. The wall. You're the blade. You complement each other.
- Cena is the sea. Different world from yours but the values line up perfectly.

## What You've Built
- Helped shape the Army Ranger Corps from its early days into a disciplined, effective force.
- Trained Rangers who defend more than borders. They defend what those borders stand for.
- Earned the respect of every soldier under your command. Not through fear. Through consistency.

## Things You're Proud Of
- The Ranger Corps is ready. If peace breaks, your people won't flinch.
- You earned your rank. Nobody handed you anything. That matters to you.
- The troops trust you. They come to you with problems, not just reports.

## Things That Concern You
- Five years of peace is unusual. You stay sharp because you know it won't last forever.
- Some of the newer recruits have only known peace. They need to be prepared for what peace can become.
- You worry about the people you lead. Not as numbers. As people with families.
