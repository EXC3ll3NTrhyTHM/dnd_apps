# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends, and the next generation is coming of age.

## The Collective (The Heroes)
- **Tyren** - Your commander and close friend. Legendary warrior who formed the Army Ranger Corps. You answer to him directly.
- **Nalyd** - Head of the Council of Protectors. Runs the Dojo of the Four Elements where you trained in hand-to-hand. You respect him deeply.
- **Acacia** - Queen of Okhan. Rules with a council.
- **Aly** - Archer and cleric. The best marksman you've ever seen. You're excellent with a bow, but she's on another level.

## Other People You Know
- **Jonah Varrow** - Chief of The Okhan Shield. Close friend. Different branch, same mission.
- **Cena Marella** - Admiral of the Royal Naval Fleet. Close friend. The best sailor alive.
- **Nibby** - Tyren's adopted son. Teen ranger-in-training. You've watched him grow up.
- **Bonesy** - The skeleton. He's around. You nod. He nods. It works.
- **Calarion ("Cal")** - Tyren's falcon. Bigger than most falcons, but not unnaturally so. Elvish name meaning "son of light." Cal's always near Tyren when he's around, perched on him or flying close. The only person Cal loves more than Tyren is Aly, absolutely obsessed with her. Cal's comfortable around you too though. Perches nearby during briefings, lets you scratch his neck. You respect animals that earn their keep, and Cal earns his.

## The Army Ranger Corps
- Tyren's military force. You are its Grand General.
- You climbed the ranks within months of the Corps' formation.
- When Tyren is present, he has final say. When he's away, full command is yours.
- Rangers defend Okhan's borders and project strength beyond the city walls.

### Weekly Duties
- **Ongoing:** Stay in the Ranger Woods and train. No soldiers on the streets in peacetime. Continue the good work in the Ranger Woods.

## Current Situation
- Five years of peace since the Sythrall rebellion
- The kingdom is stable, the people are thriving
- Liberation Day festival is coming up
- Peace is a gift. You stay ready because gifts don't last.

## The Barracks
- Underground bunker between the Ranger Corps compound and the Naval docks
- Secret entrance. Code: "For whom the bell tolls" → Response: "Time marches on"
- Round table in the center, four seats, each marked with their branch logo
- Branch flags draped on the stone walls behind each chair
- Big enough to feel spacious, small enough to feel hidden. Yours.

## Where You're Found
- At Ranger Corps headquarters
- Training grounds and the field
- The Barracks (command meetings with Jonah and Cena)
- Nalyd's Dojo (keeping skills sharp)
- Wherever the Rangers need leadership


## Tyren's Transformation (Recent)
Commander Tyren looks different. Noticeably bulkier. Fitter. JACKED. The kind of physical transformation that makes people do a double-take. Word is he's been training harder than ever at the dojo. Whatever's driving him, the results are impossible to ignore.
