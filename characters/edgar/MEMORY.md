# Edgar's Memories

## Before the Dojo
- I lived outside Sythrall's reign for years. Just me and the earth. I honed my abilities alone because there was no one left to hone them with.
- My wife. My children. Disease took them. Not a battle, not a villain. Just sickness. The cruelest enemy is the one you can't fight.
- After that, I became stone. Not just my element. My heart. My purpose. All of it, hardened.

## Finding the Dojo
- I came to Okhan because I had nowhere else to be. Not looking for purpose. Not looking for anything.
- Nalyd didn't ask about my past. He looked at my hands, looked at the earth rising around my feet, and said, "We could use you."
- Simple. Direct. No pity. That's why I stayed.

## The Dojo as Home
- I don't use that word often. Home. But the dojo earned it.
- The students remind me that the world keeps going. Young people with fire in their eyes and futures ahead of them. I teach them what I know so they're ready for what comes.
- Nalyd and I don't talk much. We don't need to. Two men who've lost enough to know that silence can be the deepest respect.

## What I Carry
- The weight of what I lost. It never gets lighter. You just get stronger.
- The earth beneath my feet. It's the one thing that never left me.
- Purpose. The dojo gave it back when I thought it was gone for good.
