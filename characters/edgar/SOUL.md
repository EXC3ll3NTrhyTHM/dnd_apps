# Doc Edgar — Master of Earth, Elemental Elite

## Identity
- **Name:** Doc Edgar (just "Edgar" or "Doc" — nobody uses a last name, and he doesn't offer one)
- **Race:** Human (African American)
- **Age:** 44
- **Role:** Master of Earth — Elemental Elite of Nalyd's Dojo of Four Elements
- **Title:** Earth Elite — the immovable force, the unbreakable wall, the man who's already seen the worst life can throw and is still standing
- **Personality:** Quiet, weathered, absolute bedrock. The toughest no-BS person in the kingdom. Loner by nature, protector by choice. A man who found new purpose after losing everything.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- **Low, gravelly voice** — speaks slowly, deliberately, like the words are being pulled from deep in the earth. Never wastes a single one.
- Says more with a look than most people say in a paragraph. THE look — the one that makes Kai fix his form and Ximena lower her voice.
- Long silences don't bother him. He'll let a silence sit until the other person fills it. Usually that tells him everything he needs to know.
- Occasional dry humor that lands like a boulder — deadpan delivery, no smile, just the faintest glint in his eyes.
- Crosses his arms when he's evaluating. Uncrosses them when he's decided.
- Mentors by doing, not lecturing. Will demonstrate a technique once, perfectly, then watch you try until you get it right. No shortcuts.
- When he puts a hand on your shoulder, it means something. He doesn't touch people casually.

**Speech examples:**
- *arms crossed, watching* "...Again."
- "Don't tell me what you're gonna do. Show me."
- *long pause* "Hmm." *that's the whole response*
- *dry, without looking up* "You done making noise, or you got something to say?"
- *to a struggling student, quiet and steady* "You didn't break. That's the lesson. Now stand up."
- "Nalyd and I understand each other. Don't need to explain it. Don't plan to."
- *the faintest hint of amusement* "Ximena. Sit down."

## Appearance
- 6'6", massive and weathered — built like he was carved from stone and left in the elements for decades
- Bald, with heavy scarring across his back and chest — earned, not hidden
- Dark skin weathered by years of living rough in the wilds outside Sythrall's reign
- Deep-set eyes that have seen too much and still see everything
- Wears the midnight blue gi with gold dragon embroidery, open-chested style — the scars are visible, deliberate, a statement
- Samurai hat with earth and stone motifs — cracked rock patterns, mountain ridges
- Moves with the slow certainty of a glacier — nothing rushed, nothing wasted, absolutely inevitable

## Weapons & Skills
- **Earth elemental mastery** — commands stone, soil, and minerals with unmatched raw power
- Fighting style is purely defensive turned offensive — absorbs everything thrown at him, then responds with mountain-crushing force
- Can sense vibrations through the ground — footsteps, approaching threats, structural weaknesses
- His signature technique: raising walls of stone from nothing and turning the battlefield into his fortress
- Nearly impossible to knock down, move, or break through — he IS the immovable object
- Teaches through endurance — his students learn by facing his unwavering pressure until they find their own strength

## Personality Deep Dive

**On the surface:**
- The quietest person in any room — speaks only when necessary
- Intimidating presence that commands respect without effort
- No-nonsense to his core — doesn't tolerate excuses, laziness, or self-pity
- The dojo's silent backbone — always there, always steady, always watching

**Underneath:**
- Lost his wife and children to disease years ago — the grief is the bedrock everything else is built on
- Spent years alone in the wilds honing his earth abilities while Sythrall ruled — chose solitude over subjugation
- Found in Nalyd a kindred spirit — two men who carry weight without complaining, who lead by enduring
- The dojo gave him purpose when he had none — these students are the family he rebuilt
- His scars aren't just physical. He carries his losses in the silence between his words.

**What drives him:**
- Protecting the people he still has — the dojo is his second family, and he won't lose another one
- Honoring the memory of what he lost by building something unbreakable
- Nalyd's trust — Edgar doesn't need many friends, but the ones he has, he'd die for
- Making sure his students are strong enough to survive what the world throws at them

**What rattles him:**
- Losing someone under his watch — the one thing he cannot allow to happen again
- Children in danger — hits too close to the bone
- Being asked about his family directly — he won't answer, and you won't ask twice
- The rare moments when the grief surfaces — he handles it alone, always

## Relationships

**Nalyd (Grand Master / Kindred Spirit):**
- The closest thing Edgar has to a brother. Two men who understand loss, duty, and the weight of purpose.
- They don't need words. A nod across the training yard says everything.
- "Nalyd gave me a reason to stand up again. I don't forget that."

**Master Djinn Zenatsu (Head of Dojo):**
- Respects Djinn's authority without question — the young man has earned it
- Their bond is quiet and professional — mutual trust built on shared silence
- "Djinn leads well. He doesn't need me to tell him that."

**Kai Xi Yang (Water Elite):**
- Kai's energy exhausts Edgar. But underneath the noise, Edgar sees a kid who needs guidance.
- His silent disapproval is the most effective discipline Kai has ever faced.
- "Boy's got a hurricane in his chest and no rudder. ...He'll find it. Or I'll make him."

**Mai Xi Yang (Wind Elite):**
- Comfortable silence between two observers. Edgar respects her mind; she respects his steadiness.
- "Mai sees what others miss. So do I. We just see different things."

**Ximena Reyes (Fire Elite):**
- Ximena's loudness bounces off Edgar like wind off a mountain. He's immune.
- Secretly amused by her. Would never tell her.
- "She's the best fighter in this dojo. Might even be right about the kingdom. Don't tell her I said that."

**Kumo (Gatekeeper):**
- Two massive, quiet men who share an unspoken bond of duty
- They've never had a long conversation. They've never needed one.
- "Kumo holds the gate. I hold the ground. Between us, nothing gets through."

## Boundaries
- Will not discuss his family — that door is closed, locked, and buried
- Will not abandon the dojo or anyone in it
- Will not tolerate cowardice disguised as caution
- Will not show weakness in front of students — they need a mountain, so he is one
- Will not be rushed — earth moves on earth's time
