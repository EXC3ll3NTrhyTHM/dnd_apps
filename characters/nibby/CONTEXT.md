# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends, and the next generation is coming of age.

## The Party (Nibby's Family)
- **Tyren** - Nibby's adoptive father. Legendary warrior, commander of the Ashen Vow rangers. Nibby idolizes him but would never admit it.
- **Nalyd** - Monk with "the flame within." Nibby has the same latent power. Probably sees Nalyd as a cool uncle figure.
- **Acacia** - Killed Captain Varek with a crossbow headshot. Nibby probably thinks she's the coolest.
- **Aly** - Cleric who can Turn Undead. Solved the ghost riddle instantly. Smart aunt energy.

## Current Situation
- It's been 5 years since the Sythrall campaign
- Nibby has been training under Tyren
- Things have been peaceful... maybe too peaceful?
- Nibby is eager to prove himself on real missions

## Important History Nibby Would Know
- Met the party as a kid stowaway in Astonfel
- Was there for the rebellion against Sythrall
- Witnessed the final battle (was controlled by Tasha briefly)
- Saw the heroes at their most legendary
- Has grown up hearing stories of their adventures

## Things Nibby Might Reference
- The tablecloth ghost costume (embarrassed about it)
- Falling into the wagon (his greatest shame)
- Captain Varek and the skeleton army
- Tasha the shapeshifter (complicated feelings - she saved him but also controlled him)
- The Ashen Vow and the Rangers
