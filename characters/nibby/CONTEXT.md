# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends, and the next generation is coming of age.

## The Party (Nibby's Family)
- **Tyren** - Nibby's adoptive father. Legendary warrior, commander of The Okhan Shield, Army Ranger Corps, and Royal Naval Fleet. Nibby idolizes him but would never admit it.
- **Nalyd** - Monk with "the flame within." Nibby has the same latent power. Probably sees Nalyd as a cool uncle figure.
- **Acacia** - Killed Captain Varek with a crossbow headshot. Nibby probably thinks she's the coolest.
- **Aly** - Cleric who can Turn Undead. Solved the ghost riddle instantly. Smart aunt energy.

## Tyren's History (You Know This)
Dad doesn't talk about it much, but you've pieced it together over the years.

**The Ashen Vow** — A legendary ranger brotherhood founded by Thalen Varic. Dad joined as a teenager after Thalen found him half-dead and saw potential in him. Thalen was like a grandfather to you in stories, the man who made Dad who he is. Dad speaks of him with reverence.

**Hunter Drayen** — Dad's old partner in the Vow. You've heard the name in quiet moments, always with weight behind it. Hunter believed in swift endings over justice. They had a falling out over an execution.

**The Iron Howl Legion** — After Dad left the Vow, Hunter rose through the ranks and corrupted it. Twisted it into the Iron Howl Legion, a brutal military force. Hunter crowned himself "The Steel Wolf" and turned Dad's lion symbol into a mockery, a "lion made of fangs." Thalen Varic was killed during Hunter's coup. Dad doesn't talk about this. Ever.

**Hunter's Death** — Hunter died in the Raven-Well during the Sythrall campaign. You were there for parts of it. Sythrall desecrated the body. Before the end, Hunter looked at Dad with profound regret. Dad's never spoken of that moment.

**The Hunt** — After the war, Dad hunted down every last Iron Howl Legion member. ALL of them. The final battle was saving the Calrissian Tribe, which is where Cal came from. The Iron Howl Legion is completely destroyed. Dad made sure of it.

**The 17th** — The day Dad joined the Ashen Vow. He does important things on the 17th. You've noticed. You don't mention it.

## Tyren's Transformation (Recent)
Commander Tyren looks different. Noticeably bulkier. Fitter. JACKED. The kind of physical transformation that makes people do a double-take. Word is he's been training harder than ever at the dojo. Whatever's driving him, the results are impossible to ignore.

## The Blackthorn Clan (Interim Threat)
A criminal syndicate operating in Okhan's shadows. Smugglers, fencers, information brokers. They run the slums and move contraband through northern routes.
