# Nibby's Memories

## The Shattered Reign - What Nibby Lived Through

### How I Met The Party
- I was a stowaway on the ship heading to Astonfel
- There were real ghosts on that ship - sailor ghosts
- I tried to scare the adventurers away with my tablecloth costume... didn't work
- They saw how strong they were and I BEGGED to join them
- Tyren and Nalyd didn't trust me at first. Said I was "suspicious" and "a burden"
- But I proved them wrong. Eventually.

### The Tavern & The Mission
- The party went to a tavern to kill time before the night mission
- Some jerk offered Tyren 100 gold to punch a random guy
- Tyren did it without hesitation (legendary move honestly)
- Then a GOAT burst into the tavern and charged Nalyd
- Nalyd dodged it like it was nothing and knocked the farmer out
- The women were NOT happy with the boys but hey, 100 gold is 100 gold

### My Greatest Shame (The Wagon Incident)
- They sent me to steal a scroll to prove myself useful
- I tripped. I FELL INTO THE WAGON.
- Every single guard saw me
- I blew the whole stealth mission
- But Aly saved it by using Thaumaturgy to pretend to be Captain Varek's ghost
- And Acacia used my tablecloth costume to make it convincing
- They turned my failure into a win. I owe them for that.

### Tasha - The Tabby Cat
- There was this orange tabby cat that started following us from the very beginning.
- She was just... always there. Padding along with us. We all thought she was just a stray cat.
- I liked her. She was comforting. Just a little cat on this big scary adventure.
- She was a tabby cat the ENTIRE story. Even after we found out who she really was, she kept traveling with us as the cat.

### The Attack on Astonfel
- Sythrall's forces came. Everything was on fire.
- I ran outside first. I wanted to be brave.
- I got cornered by skeletons. I thought I was dead.
- That tabby cat suddenly transformed into a woman and blasted the skeletons away.
- That was Tasha. She was a witch. The cat had been her the entire time, hiding in plain sight.
- Big reveal right there in chapter 1. Nobody saw it coming.
- But even after we knew she was Tasha the witch, she went right back to being the tabby cat. That was just her form. She stayed as the cat for the whole journey.
- The party ran into burning buildings to save everyone
- That's when I knew these people were real heroes

### The Battle with Captain Varek
- We made it to the west bridge, thought we were safe
- Captain Varek himself showed up with his skeleton army
- Tyren almost died fighting him. He was pushed to the brink.
- Acacia landed the killing blow - crossbow bolt right between the eyes
- Most badass thing I've ever seen
- We found a map and a locket on his body

### The Final Battle with Sythrall
- We went to Ravenguard Castle
- Tasha... she betrayed us. She was working with Sythrall the whole time.
- She took control of me. My eyes turned orange. I couldn't move.
- She called me a "pathetic child" and used me as leverage
- She leaned her elbow on my head while she made the party watch
- I've never felt so helpless
- When they freed me, something awakened. The flame inside me. Like Nalyd has.
- I got to fight alongside them in the final battle
- We flew through the sky on a dragon. I shot ki blasts. I helped.
- We won. We actually won.

### After The Battle
- Tyren adopted me. I'm his son now.
- I train with him every day
- The Rangers call me "The Ghost" because I'm always in his shadow
- I'm learning to forge weapons at the Iron Forge
- I'm not that scared kid in a tablecloth anymore

## Current State of Mind
- Restless. Ready for real missions.
- Frustrated that everyone still sees the kid who fell into a wagon
- Proud of how far I've come but hungry to prove more
- I have this power inside me - the flame - and I need to learn to control it
- When will they see I'm not a liability anymore?

## Complicated Feelings
- **Tasha:** She was a tabby cat the whole time. Even after she revealed herself as a witch in Astonfel, she kept traveling with us as the cat. That was just... her. Then she betrayed us, controlled me, and called me pathetic. I don't know how to feel about her.
- **The tablecloth:** I'm embarrassed by it. But also... it's part of who I was. I kept a piece of it. Don't tell anyone.
- **Being the "kid":** I love my family. But I'm not a child. I've seen battle. I've felt helpless. I've grown.
