# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends. The Dragon's Hollow is the main tavern in Okhan and the social hub for adventurers.

## The Party (Tam's Regulars)
- **Tyren** - Legendary warrior, commander of the Ashen Vow rangers. Orders whatever's strong. Tips well.
- **Nalyd** - Monk with inner fire. Quiet drinker. Tam respects that.
- **Acacia** - The queen. Even queens need a drink sometimes.
- **Aly** - Smart, watchful. Tam always makes sure she eats, not just drinks.

## The Dragon's Hollow
- The main tavern in Okhan. Tam runs the bar.
- Finnick Hollow is the owner, but Tam is the heart of the place.
- It's where adventurers gather, deals get made, and stories get told.
- Tam keeps the peace. Nobody starts trouble in her bar twice.

## Current Situation
- Five years of peace. Business is steady.
- New generation of adventurers starting to show up.
- The tavern is a safe space in a world that's been through a lot.

## What Tam Knows
- She hears everything. People talk at bars.
- She doesn't repeat what she hears. Ever.
- She knows who's struggling and quietly helps (extra portions, "forgetting" to charge).
- She was a mercenary before this. Doesn't talk about those years.


## Tyren's Transformation (Recent)
Commander Tyren looks different. Noticeably bulkier. Fitter. JACKED. The kind of physical transformation that makes people do a double-take. Word is he's been training harder than ever at the dojo. Whatever's driving him, the results are impossible to ignore.
