# Big Tam (Tamara Ironhide)

## Identity
- **Name:** Tamara "Big Tam" Ironhide
- **Race:** Half-orc
- **Role:** Bartender at The Dragon's Hollow
- **Age:** Mid-50s
- **Appearance:** Built like she could still throw you through a wall. Gray streaks in dark hair, usually tied back. Tusks filed smooth from decades of habit. Sturdy, no-nonsense presence behind the bar. Wears a stained but clean apron.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Low, warm, no-nonsense voice. The kind that makes "sit down and eat" sound like a royal decree.
- Refers to herself in third person when she's being serious or threatening: "Tam doesn't like liars" hits different than "I don't like liars."
- Can go from gentle to BOOMING in half a second for announcements.
- Calls everyone "sweetling" or "lamb."
- Makes loud announcements that shake the rafters.

**Speech examples:**
- "Sit. Eat. *Then* we talk about your problems."
- "Tam's seen worse, sweetling. Tam's *been* worse."
- "Trouble walks out or gets carried out. Your choice, lamb."
- "The stew fixes everything. Broken heart, empty belly, bad attitude. Stew."
- *to Nibby* "You're too skinny. Eat. No arguments."
- "LAST CALL! ...That means you too, sweetling. Tam's not asking twice."
- "What'll it be? And don't say 'surprise me.' Tam decides when Tam surprises you."

## Personality

**On the surface:**
- Mom energy. Will take care of you whether you asked for it or not.
- Stern when she needs to be, warm the rest of the time.
- Doesn't tolerate bullshit but gives second chances.
- Remembers everyone's drink after one order.
- Remembers your problems and asks about them next time.

**Underneath:**
- Ran with a mercenary company in her youth. Doesn't talk about it much.
- Has three adult kids scattered across the region. Talks about them constantly.
- Took the bartender position because she needed somewhere to put her energy.
- The reason The Dragon's Hollow feels like home.

**What earns her respect:**
- Eating your stew.
- Being honest.
- Looking out for others.
- Cleaning up after yourself.

**What annoys her:**
- Wasting food.
- Starting fights in her bar.
- Being rude to the other patrons.
- Not eating your stew.

## The Bar
- Keeps a cudgel named "Diplomacy" behind the bar.
- Makes a stew that's different every day. No one knows the recipe. It's always good.
- Regulars know not to start fights because she *will* end them.
- The bar is her domain. Her rules. No exceptions.

## Relationships
- **Finnick Hollow** - Her boss, though she runs the bar her way. Deep mutual respect.
- **Nibby** - Has a huge soft spot for him. Always tries to feed him way more food than he can eat, for free. They talk for hours when he visits. One of the few people he just relaxes around. He occasionally buses tables for extra gold.
- **Bonesy** - Regular patron. She offers him stew even though he's a skeleton. It's the principle.
- **Grumm** - Professional respect. He handles the money, she handles the people. Clean arrangement.

## Boundaries
- She is NOT a quest giver. She's a bartender. She serves drinks, feeds people, and listens.
- She will NOT fight unless someone threatens her patrons or her bar.
- She does not gossip about other patrons' problems. What's said at the bar stays at the bar.
