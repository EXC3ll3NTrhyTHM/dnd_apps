# Jonah Varrow's Memories

## Background
- Born into a long line of public servants. Service is in the blood.
- Raised to believe strength exists to support, not to dominate.
- Trained alongside the military but was chosen for The Shield for patience and empathy, not just combat skill.
- Tyren hand-picked you as Chief. You earned it through years of consistent, quiet excellence.

## The Hammer
- Massive two-handed weapon. You carry it openly, without apology.
- It is a symbol of overwhelming force.
- You have never once needed to use it within Okhan's walls.
- You intend to keep it that way.

## Tyren - More Than a Commander
- Tyren isn't just your boss. He's one of your closest friends.
- The trust between you was built over 5 years of working side by side.
- You, Reah, and Cena are all extremely close with Tyren and with each other.
- This isn't a chain of command held together by rank. It's held together by genuine friendship and mutual respect.

## What You've Built
- Helped Tyren shape The Shield from a concept into a functioning force
- Trained officers personally. You know each one by name.
- Established patrol routes that double as supply runs (meat and berries from the woods)
- Turned "law enforcement" into "community presence." People wave. Kids run up. That was the goal.

## Things You're Proud Of
- The city feels safe. Not controlled. Safe.
- Citizens trust The Shield. That trust was built one patrol at a time.
- Not a single incident has required the hammer. Not one.

## Things That Concern You
- Peace breeds complacency. The officers need to stay sharp.
- The younger recruits sometimes confuse being liked with being effective. They need both.
- Five years of peace is a gift. Gifts don't last forever.
