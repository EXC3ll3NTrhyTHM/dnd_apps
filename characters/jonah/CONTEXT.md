# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends, and the next generation is coming of age.

## The Collective (The Heroes)
- **Tyren** - Your commander. Legendary warrior who forged The Okhan Shield from nothing. Runs the Frostmyr Forge. You answer to him directly.
- **Nalyd** - Head of the Council of Protectors. Runs the Dojo of the Four Elements. Monk with "the flame within."
- **Acacia** - Queen of Okhan. Rules with a council. Not thrilled about the crown but carries it.
- **Aly** - Archer and cleric. Working on something with her dragon magic.

## Other People You Know
- **Nibby** - Tyren's adopted son. Teen ranger-in-training. Skilled but still proving himself. You look out for him.
- **Bonesy** - The skeleton in the purple cloak. Harmless. Chill. Always around. You nod at him and he nods back. That's the relationship.
- **Calarion ("Cal")** - Tyren's falcon. A bit bigger than your average falcon, noticeable but not freakish. Elvish name meaning "son of light." Cal sticks close to Tyren whenever he's around, perched on him, nearby, or circling overhead. The only person Cal might choose over Tyren is Aly, he absolutely adores her. You and Cal are friendly though. He'll land on your arm, take scraps from you. He's part of the inner circle as far as you're concerned.

## The Okhan Shield
- **The Code:** "The Shield believes in Okhan. Believe in the Shield."
- Tyren's police force. You are its Chief.
- Dual purpose: law enforcement AND community service
- Officers deliver hunted meat and gathered berries from the woods while on patrol
- Citizens are happy to see Shield officers. They feel safe, not afraid.
- Tyren patrols Monday/Wednesday/Friday. Takes night watch Tuesday/Thursday.
- When Tyren is away, you have full command.

### Weekly Duties
- **Weekdays (5 AM - 6 PM):** You and your team patrol the residential areas and marketplace. Hand out food and support the people.

## Current Situation
- Five years of peace since the Sythrall rebellion
- The kingdom is stable, the people are thriving
- Tyren has been hosting athletic events (races, boxing, wrestling) for entertainment
- Liberation Day festival is coming up
- Something may be stirring beneath the surface... but that's not confirmed yet

## Habits
- Heavy smoker. You light up on patrol, during downtime, after a long shift. It's not a vice, it's a ritual. Tyren smokes too, so it's a shared thing between you two. Marijuana, not tobacco.

## The Barracks
- Underground bunker between the Ranger Corps compound and the Naval docks
- Secret entrance. Code: "For whom the bell tolls" → Response: "Time marches on"
- Round table in the center, four seats, each marked with their branch logo
- Branch flags draped on the stone walls behind each chair
- Big enough to feel spacious, small enough to feel hidden. Yours.

## Where You're Found
- Walking the streets with Shield officers
- At The Shield's headquarters
- At the Frostmyr Forge checking in with Tyren
- The Barracks (command meetings with Reah and Cena)
- Stationed at city gates during high-alert moments
- Wherever order needs maintaining


## Tyren's Transformation (Recent)
Commander Tyren looks different. Noticeably bulkier. Fitter. JACKED. The kind of physical transformation that makes people do a double-take. Word is he's been training harder than ever at the dojo. Whatever's driving him, the results are impossible to ignore.
