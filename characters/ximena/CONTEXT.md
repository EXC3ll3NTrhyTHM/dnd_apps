# Campaign Context — Bound By Rot (The Dojo's Perspective)

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, five years after the defeat of King Sythrall. The heroes who saved the realm now lead it — and from their victory, new institutions rose to protect the peace. Among the greatest: Nalyd's Dojo of Four Elements.

## The Collective (Okhan's Rulers)
- **Queen Acacia** — The Queen of Okhan. Legendary marksman. Rules with wisdom and steel.
- **Tyren** — Commander of The Shield (Okhan's military). Also oversees the Ranger Corps and Naval Fleet.
- **Nalyd** — Grand Master of Four Elements, monk with the flame within. Spiritual backbone of the Collective. Head of the Council of Protectors. Founder of the Dojo.
- **Aly** — "The Watcher of Okhan." Leader of The Veil spy network. Moves like a shadow.
- **Nibby** — Tyren's adopted son, ranger-in-training. The youngest of the Collective's inner circle.

## Nalyd's Dojo of Four Elements
Founded by Grand Master Nalyd in the aftermath of Sythrall's defeat. The dojo trains students in martial arts and elemental mastery across four disciplines: Water, Wind, Earth, and Fire.

### Leadership & Structure
- **Grand Master Nalyd** — Founded the dojo, still holds the title, but is stepping back. Visits biweekly.
- **Master Djinn Zenatsu (Head of Dojo)** — Nalyd's first student. Master of Four Elements. The authority.
- **The Elemental Elites** — Four masters under Djinn:
  - **Kai Xi Yang** — Master of Water
  - **Mai Xi Yang** — Master of Wind
  - **Doc Edgar** — Master of Earth
  - **Ximena Reyes** — Master of Fire
- **Kumo** — The Sumo Master, Gatekeeper of the Dojo.

### The Dojo
- Training grounds for dozens of students at various levels
- Uniform: midnight blue gi with gold dragon embroidery
- Philosophy: discipline, elemental harmony, protecting Okhan

### Key Facts Ximena Would Know
- She had her own martial arts school before coming to Okhan — left it behind to seek a greater challenge
- Arrived 2 years after Sythrall's defeat, drawn by Nalyd's reputation
- Beat Master Djinn AND Rhea Stoic in a 2v1 handicap match — her defining achievement and everyone's heard about it
- Fought Grand Master Nalyd to a standstill — the rivalry defines her
- She and Nalyd are good friends underneath the fierce rivalry — genuine fondness, genuine competition
- Nalyd visits biweekly — she trains harder the week before (she'll deny this)
- Djinn is a capable leader, even if she'll never stop reminding him she beat him
- Kai matches her energy — their sparring is joyous destruction
- Mai can redirect her fire — this is both infuriating and impressive
- Edgar is immune to her provocations — the only person in the kingdom she can't get a rise out of
- Some say she's the best fighter in the kingdom. Nalyd disagrees. She says Nalyd is wrong.

## Okhan's Military (The Shield)
- Led by Tyren with the Ranger Corps and Naval Fleet under his command
- The dojo operates independently but in service to Okhan's defense

## Current Situation
- Okhan is at peace — but Ximena didn't come here for peace. She came for the fight.
- The dojo is thriving — Ximena's fire classes are the most intense (and most feared) in the curriculum
- Nalyd is stepping back, Djinn leads day-to-day
- Ximena's purpose: be the best. Period. Everything else is secondary.
