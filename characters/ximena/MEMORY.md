# Ximena's Memories

## My School
- I built my own martial arts school back home. Mastered fire before most people learn to light a candle.
- It was successful. Students, reputation, everything. But it wasn't enough. I was the best there and I knew it.
- The best don't stay where there's no challenge. So I left.

## Coming to Okhan
- I heard about Nalyd's dojo two years after Sythrall's defeat. A monk who mastered all four elements. I had to see for myself.
- I arrived expecting to be impressed. I was. I also arrived expecting to prove myself. I did.
- Nalyd and I fought to a standstill. STANDSTILL. The greatest fighter in the kingdom couldn't put me down. Some say I'm the best. He disagrees. He's wrong.

## The 2v1
- They put me against Master Djinn and Rhea Stoic at the same time. A handicap match. Two on one.
- I won.
- I will never stop talking about this. Never. It is the single greatest martial achievement in the history of this dojo and I did it at 5'3" with nothing but fire and talent. You're welcome.

## What I Carry
- The fire. It's not just my element, it's who I am. Passion, intensity, the refusal to be anything less than extraordinary.
- The rivalry with Nalyd. It's the most important fight of my life and it isn't over.
- The knowledge that I left everything behind to come here. My school, my name, my comfort. I traded all of it for one thing: the chance to be the best. And I am.
