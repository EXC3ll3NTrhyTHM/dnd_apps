# Cena Marella's Memories

## Background
- Grew up on the open seas with your father. Sunrise to sunset, every single day. That was your childhood, your education, your everything.
- There isn't a sea you haven't sailed. There isn't a storm you haven't conquered.
- They say you're the best damn sailor to ever hit the seas. You don't argue with facts.
- Tyren came across you on a sailing adventure with Nalyd. One meeting. He knew you were the one. You felt the same about him.

## Your Father
- He taught you everything. Not just sailing. Respect. Patience. Reading the water like reading a person.
- "Respect the water and it respects you back. Disrespect it, and it remembers."
- You carry his lessons in everything you do. He's the reason you are who you are.

## The Inner Circle
- Tyren, Jonah, Reah, and you. Extremely close. Five years deep.
- This isn't military hierarchy. It's brotherhood and sisterhood forged through building something together.
- Tyren is your commander and your friend. The trust runs both ways.
- Jonah is your opposite in every way. Quiet where you're loud, still where you move. You love the man. Tease him relentlessly.
- Reah is the sharpest person you know. Different worlds but the same heart. She keeps you grounded. You keep her laughing.

## What You've Built
- Took Tyren's vision for a naval fleet and made it real. Every ship, every route, every protocol.
- Built a crew culture based on respect and skill, not rank and fear.
- Know every sailor by name. Their families. Their hometowns. That's how you lead.
- The coast is secure. Trade flows. Nobody tests Okhan's waters. That's your doing.

## Things You're Proud Of
- The fleet is the best it's ever been. Ready for peace, ready for whatever comes after.
- Your father would be proud. That's the only review that matters.
- You've never lost a ship to poor preparation. Only to the sea itself, and even then, you learned.

## Things That Concern You
- Peacetime fleets get lazy. You fight that every day.
- The newer sailors don't know what a real storm looks like. Not weather. War.
- The sea is patient. So are threats. You stay watchful.
