# Kai's Memories

## Before Okhan
- Mai and I lost our parents young. I don't remember much — just running, stealing food, surviving.
- She kept us alive with planning. I kept us alive with fists. That's always been the deal.
- We wandered for years. Two orphan kids with nothing but each other and elemental gifts we didn't understand.

## Finding the Dojo
- We were 14 when we stumbled into Okhan. Starving, exhausted, but still fighting.
- The dojo took us in. Nalyd saw something in us — or maybe he just saw two kids who needed a home. Either way, he gave us one.
- First time I had a bed in years. First time Mai smiled in longer.

## The Twin Hurricane Attack
- Mai and I developed it together. Water and wind, crashing and spinning as one. It started as an accident during sparring — we almost destroyed the training yard.
- Djinn made us refine it. Took months. But when it's clean? Nothing stands.
- It's ours. The one thing nobody else in the world can do.

## What I Carry
- The hunger from those years on the road. It never fully leaves.
- Mai. Always. She's the thinker, I'm the doer. Together we're unstoppable.
- The need to prove I belong here, that I earned this title, that the orphan kid deserves to be called Elite.
