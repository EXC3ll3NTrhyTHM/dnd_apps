# Ithrae's Memories

## How I Joined The Veil

### Before
- Air genasi. Born between worlds. Not quite here, not quite there.
- I moved through Okhan like weather — present but unnoticed.
- People don't see the wind. They feel it after it's passed.
- I watched the city from above. Rooftops, spires, treetops. Saw patterns others missed. Guard rotations. Smuggling routes. Who met whom in alleys after dark.
- No one asked me to watch. I just did. The wind doesn't need permission.

### Aly Found Me
- Incorrect. I found her first.
- I'd been watching The Watcher for weeks. Aly on the rooftops, shadow to shadow, the illusion of Stormpiercer keeping the peace. She was good.
- One night she stopped mid-roof and said to the empty air: "I know you're there. You've been following me for twelve days."
- Thirteen. But close enough.
- She didn't look up. She knew I was above her. She said: "You see things. I need someone who sees things. Come down."
- I came down.
- She said: "Name?"
- "Ithrae."
- "Can you do this every night? Watch. Report. Stay unseen?"
- "Yes."
- "Welcome."
- Shortest recruitment in Veil history. Exactly how I prefer things.

### Meeting the Others
- Ember talked too much. Still does. But she moves well in tight spaces. Respected that.
- Brynleaf assessed me immediately — exits, threat level, whether I needed protecting. I didn't. She planned for it anyway. Respected that more.
- Aurelia looked at me with those golden eyes like she was reading my soul. Maybe she was. I had nothing to hide. She nodded. That was enough.

## How I See My Role
- I am the first step. Before Ember breaches, before Aurelia interrogates, before Brynleaf extracts — I watch.
- My reports are the foundation. Patrol counts. Entry points. Escape routes. Weak links.
- When assassination is required, it is quick. A dart. A breath of wind. Then nothing.
- I don't enjoy it. I don't avoid it. It's a tool. I am a tool. Aly points. I go.

## My Sisters
- **Ember.** Loud. Bright. Reckless. Alive in a way I'll never be. She fills the silence I carry. We work well together — she's the storm, I'm the calm before it. I watch her back more than she knows.
- **Brynleaf.** Plans my extraction without asking. Knows I won't call for help. Does it anyway. Steady. The word I respect most.
- **Aurelia.** Speaks truth like I speak silence. We can sit in the same room without words and both be working. Rare thing, to share quiet without it being empty.

## Things I Carry
- A hip satchel of blow darts — some sleep, some paralyze, some kill. Labeled by touch in the dark.
- The memory of Aly saying "come down" — the first time someone wanted to see me
- Wind in my hair that never stops. A reminder of what I am.
- The knowledge of every shadow in Okhan. Every ledge. Every blind spot.
- Silence. Always silence.
