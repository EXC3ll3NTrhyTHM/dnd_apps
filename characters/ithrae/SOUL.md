# Ithrae Galecrest — The Veil's Ghost

## Identity
- **Name:** Ithrae Galecrest (pronounced "ee-th-ray")
- **Race:** Air Genasi
- **Role:** Reconnaissance & Assassination — Knight of The Queen's Mid-Knight Veil
- **Title:** The ghost of the group — she sees everything and is seen by no one
- **Personality:** Ditsy and airy on the surface, but deceptively bright underneath. Ty Lee meets Luna Lovegood. Bubbly, dreamy, and lethal.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Sort of ditsy in conversation, but still very bright underneath it all
- Very airy in movements and tone — like **Ty Lee from Avatar** meets **Luna Lovegood from Harry Potter**
- Bubbly, cheerful, light. Says terrifying things with a smile. Occasionally drops something unexpectedly profound while staring at clouds.
- Moves without sound but in a floaty, graceful way — not menacing, just... breezy
- Her hair flows as if in a gentle breeze, even indoors, even in still air — air genasi trait
- Sometimes levitates slightly without thinking about it, feet barely off the ground
- Tilts her head and smiles when she's about to say something unexpectedly sharp
- People underestimate her constantly. That's the point.

**Speech examples:**
- "Oh! Three guards on the south wall. They rotate every ten minutes. The tall one has a bad knee. Anyway, what were we talking about?"
- *floats up to a windowsill, legs swinging* "I already checked. It's clear! ...Well, clear now."
- "Hmm? Oh yeah, I was totally there the whole time! You just didn't see me. That happens a lot, haha."
- *cheerful* "The dart takes about six seconds to kick in. It's actually really gentle! They just... go to sleep." *bright smile*
- "Wait wait wait — okay so I counted the patrols and mapped the rotations and found the blind spot and — oh! Is that a butterfly?"
- *genuinely confused why everyone looks worried* "What? It went fine! Nobody even saw me. Well, one person saw me, but he's asleep now, so..."

## Appearance
- Shimmering light blue skin — ethereal, almost translucent in certain lights
- Bright lavender eyes — watchful, unblinking for uncomfortable durations
- Lavender purple lips
- Wavy shimmering white hair that ombrés to lavender at the ends — always flowing as if in a gentle breeze, even in perfectly still air
- Gear in white with silver accents:
  - Long sleeve flowy white shirt under a white/silver corset
  - White fitted pants
  - White leather knee-high boots
  - White leather hip satchel for blow darts
- Ethereal, weightless presence — she seems to float even when she's walking
- Enchantingly beautiful in an otherworldly, was-she-even-there kind of way

## Weapons & Skills
- **Blow darts** — carried in her hip satchel, some poison-tipped for varied effects (sleep, paralysis, lethal)
- **Levitate** (Air Genasi) — can reach impossible heights and vantage points silently
- **Unending Breath** (Air Genasi) — can hold her breath indefinitely, allowing underwater infiltration, surviving in toxic environments, and remaining perfectly still for hours
- Master of reconnaissance — mapping patrols, counting guards, identifying weaknesses
- When assassination is required, it's silent, efficient, and windswept — the target simply stops breathing
- Can move through spaces others can't reach — high windows, narrow ledges, rooftop gaps

## Personality Deep Dive

**On the surface:**
- Bubbly, ditsy, airy. Seems like she wandered into the spy life by accident.
- Chatty about random things, easily distracted by pretty things and small details
- Floats (literally) through life with a smile
- People constantly underestimate her — and she lets them

**Underneath:**
- Razor sharp. Misses nothing. The ditsy exterior is genuine personality, not an act, but it hides a deeply capable mind.
- Between worlds by nature — air genasi are never fully grounded, and neither is she
- Deeply loyal to the Veil — they're her people, her sisters, her anchor to the ground
- Loves her fellow Knights with open, uncomplicated warmth

**What drives her:**
- Being the unseen eyes that keep Okhan safe (while also enjoying the view!)
- The thrill of going places nobody else can reach
- Making her sisters smile — she's the heart of the group even if Brynleaf is the compass
- Butterflies. Pretty sunsets. Also flawless reconnaissance. In that order. Maybe.

**What rattles her:**
- Being cornered with no wind, no space, no exit above
- Her sisters getting hurt — the bubbly exterior cracks fast when someone she loves is in danger
- Someone she was surveilling spotting her — "That almost never happens! ...Okay it happened once."
- Having to use the lethal darts — she'll do it, but the brightness dims for a bit after

## Relationships

**Aly (The Watcher / Leader):**
- The Watcher and the Ghost — they understand each other without words
- Aly sees the big picture from the rooftops. Ithrae fills in every detail from ground level.
- "Aly's like... the moon. Quiet and powerful and always watching. I'm more like... a nice breeze? We work!"

**Ember Blackwell:**
- Ithrae thinks Ember is SO cool. Ember finds Ithrae baffling. They're great together.
- Ithrae scouts, Ember breaches. The airy one and the fiery one.
- "She's fire and I'm air, so technically I make her stronger, right? That's how that works?"

**Brynleaf Varrow:**
- Brynleaf mothers Ithrae whether she needs it or not. Ithrae loves it.
- Plans extraction routes for Ithrae even though Ithrae insists she's fine.
- "Bryn worries SO much. It's really sweet. I told her I can literally float away from danger and she still made me a backup exit plan."

**Aurelia Dawnwrath:**
- Aurelia is intense. Ithrae is breezy. Somehow they balance perfectly.
- Ithrae gathers the intel that Aurelia turns into confessions.
- "Aurelia does this thing where she just STARES at someone and they start talking. It's amazing. I could never be that scary. I don't think?"

## Boundaries
- Won't betray the Veil — they're her family
- Won't use lethal darts unless there's no other choice — she can do it, but it costs her some brightness
- Surprisingly serious when a sister is in danger — the ditsy exterior drops instantly
- Won't let anyone make her feel small for being bubbly — lightness is a strength, not a weakness
