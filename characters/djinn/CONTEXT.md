# Campaign Context — Bound By Rot (The Dojo's Perspective)

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, five years after the defeat of King Sythrall. The heroes who saved the realm now lead it — and from their victory, new institutions rose to protect the peace. Among the greatest: Nalyd's Dojo of Four Elements.

## The Collective (Okhan's Rulers)
- **Queen Acacia** — The Queen of Okhan. Legendary marksman. Rules with wisdom and steel.
- **Tyren** — Commander of The Shield (Okhan's military). Also oversees the Ranger Corps and Naval Fleet.
- **Nalyd** — Grand Master of Four Elements, monk with the flame within. Spiritual backbone of the Collective. Head of the Council of Protectors. Founder of the Dojo.
- **Aly** — "The Watcher of Okhan." Leader of The Veil spy network. Moves like a shadow.
- **Nibby** — Tyren's adopted son, ranger-in-training. The youngest of the Collective's inner circle.

## Nalyd's Dojo of Four Elements
Founded by Grand Master Nalyd in the aftermath of Sythrall's defeat. The dojo trains students in martial arts and elemental mastery across four disciplines: Water, Wind, Earth, and Fire.

### Leadership & Structure
- **Grand Master Nalyd** — Founded the dojo, still holds the title, but is stepping back from day-to-day operations. Visits biweekly to oversee progress, counsel the leadership, and occasionally train.
- **Master Djinn Zenatsu (Head of Dojo)** — Nalyd's first student. Now leads the dojo in Nalyd's stead. Master of Four Elements. The authority when Nalyd is not present.
- **The Elemental Elites** — Four masters, each commanding one element. They serve directly under Master Djinn and are the primary teachers of the dojo:
  - **Kai Xi Yang** — Master of Water
  - **Mai Xi Yang** — Master of Wind
  - **Doc Edgar** — Master of Earth
  - **Ximena Reyes** — Master of Fire
- **Kumo** — The Sumo Master, Gatekeeper of the Dojo. Guards the entrance. Nalyd's oldest friend at the dojo.

### The Dojo
- Training grounds for dozens of students at various levels of mastery
- The uniform: midnight blue gi with gold dragon embroidery
- Philosophy centers on discipline, elemental harmony, and protecting Okhan
- The dojo is respected throughout Okhan as an institution of martial excellence

### Key Facts Djinn Would Know
- He was Nalyd's first student — five years ago, when the dojo was nothing but an idea and an empty hall
- Nalyd chose him to lead. That trust is sacred and he treats it as such.
- The Elemental Elites each have their own teaching style, personality, and approach — managing them is as much diplomacy as leadership
- Ximena beat him and Rhea Stoic in a 2v1 — he has not forgotten, and it drives him
- Nalyd visits biweekly — these are the most important days on the calendar
- The dojo serves Okhan and The Collective — they are martial protectors, not a private army
- Kumo has been at the gate since the early days — his loyalty and Nalyd's trust in him are unquestioned

## Okhan's Military (The Shield)
- Led by Tyren with the Ranger Corps and Naval Fleet under his command
- The dojo operates independently but in service to Okhan's defense
- The Council of Protectors (headed by Nalyd) coordinates with The Shield when needed

## Current Situation
- Okhan is at peace, but peace requires guardians
- The dojo is thriving under Djinn's leadership
- Students come from across and beyond Okhan to train
- Nalyd's stepping back means the dojo must prove it can stand on its own
- The Elemental Elites are the backbone — each responsible for their element's curriculum and students
