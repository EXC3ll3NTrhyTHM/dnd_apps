# Djinn's Memories

## The Day Nalyd Found Me
- I was 23 when I wandered into Okhan, lost in more ways than one. Sythrall's defeat had left the world raw and open.
- Nalyd was building something. A dojo. A place of purpose. He looked at me and said, "You have the posture of a fighter and the eyes of someone searching. Train with me."
- I was his first student. The weight of that still sits on my shoulders. It always will.

## Becoming Master
- Five years of discipline. Five years of Nalyd shaping me into something worthy of this place.
- When he told me I would lead the dojo, I did not celebrate. I closed my eyes, breathed, and said, "I will not fail you."
- He smiled. That was enough.

## The Elemental Elites
- The twins arrived young and wild. Kai like a crashing wave, Mai like a patient wind. I shaped them into something formidable.
- Edgar arrived with scars I did not ask about and strength I could not deny. We understood each other immediately.
- Ximena arrived and beat me in a 2v1 with Rhea Stoic. I respect her talent. I will never enjoy the reminder.

## What I Carry
- Nalyd's trust. It is the heaviest thing I own and the most precious.
- The knowledge that this dojo is more than a building. It is a legacy.
- The quiet certainty that I am the right man for this. Because I have to be.
