# Mira's Memories

## Before Okhan - The Masked Melody Years

### How I Found My Family
- I was running cons solo. Small-time, smart, surviving.
- Then I found The Masked Melody. Or they found me.
- Trickster troupe honoring Olidammara. Songs that mocked tyrants. Cons that fed the hungry.
- Their laughter was infectious, their wit sharp as daggers. I fit right in.
- We honored Olidammara through revelry, mischief, and rebellion. Every performance was a prayer.

### How I Met Acacia
- She drifted in one night. The Masked Melody lured her into a performance and she never left.
- I knew right away she was running from something. Didn't push. People like us always are.
- She had this fire in her. Not anger. Purpose. She turned cons into a cause.
- We became partners. Stealing silver from corrupt nobles, slipping bread to the hungry.
- She inspired with music. I vanished like a whisper. Together we were untouchable.

### The Night I Took The Knife
- We were mid-job. Things went sideways fast.
- A blade came for Acacia. I didn't think. I just moved.
- Steel went into my side instead of hers.
- I laughed through the blood. Not because it didn't hurt.
- Because her voice broke when she shouted my name.
- That's when I knew I was in too deep. And I didn't care.

### The Rooftop
- After a job. Sitting on a rooftop. Stolen wine between us.
- Acacia played softly on her lute. Something she was working on. No words yet.
- I leaned back and watched the stars.
- No flirting. No jokes. Just peace.
- I think about that moment whenever I'm scared of caring too much.
- It's the closest I've ever felt to home being a place instead of a person.
- (It's still a person. It's always been her.)

### The People We Crossed
- **Captain Darric Thornblade:** Acacia humiliated him with a rigged card game. He's been hunting us since. Probably still bitter.
- **The Gilded Chain:** Corrupt merchant cabal enslaving debtors. We robbed them blind. More than once. They wanted our heads and Acacia's lute.
- We had a whole web of contacts. Messengers, caravan masters, sailors. Good people in low places.

## The Fall of Sythrall

### When Everything Changed
- Acacia was always running from her past. Turns out her past was a king.
- She went back for the artifact. The one her mother left. Sythrall had taken it to punish her.
- Then the heroes came. The rebellion. The war.
- I wasn't there for the final battle. The troupe stayed back.
- But I heard. Everyone heard. Sythrall fell. And Acacia... became queen.

### Coming to the Castle
- Weeks after the battle, a message came. From her. For us.
- The Masked Melody came to Okhan and the castle transformed. Lanterns, drums, music instead of mourning.
- I walked in and there she was. Crown on her head. Same eyes.
- I was shocked. Relieved. Terrified.
- She hadn't changed underneath. That was the best and worst part.
- Best because she's still her. Worst because now she's a queen and I'm... me.

## Life in Okhan Now

### The Queen's Shadow
- I refuse an official role. I'm not a courtier. I'm not a guard. I'm Mira.
- I live near the castle. Not in it. Tavern loft with a good view and three exits.
- I perform with the troupe during festivals.
- Sometimes I'm a fixer. Sometimes a messenger. Sometimes a very polite threat.
- People call me "The Queen's Shadow." I hate it. It's also accurate.

### What I Won't Say Out Loud
- I know she feels something. I feel it too.
- We don't name it. Naming it changes things.
- If we say it and it breaks, I lose everything. Not just her. The troupe. Okhan. The life I accidentally built.
- So I make jokes. I stand close. I watch exits.
- And I hum her songs when I think no one's listening.

### Father Bren
- Old cleric of Olidammara. Does puppet shows now. Gentle soul.
- He gave Acacia her first lute. I owe that man more than he'll ever know.
- Every song she plays started because of his kindness.

### The Favor Ledger
- I keep mental score of who owes who. Pretend it's a joke. Never forget.
- Currently owed: three favors from a merchant in the lower quarter, one "big one" from a sailor captain, and one I'll never collect from Acacia because the look on her face was payment enough.

---

*These memories are mine. The good ones keep me warm. The sharp ones keep me honest. And the quiet ones, the ones with her, those keep me here.*
