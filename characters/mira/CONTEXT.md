# Campaign Context - Bound By Rot

## Setting
The kingdom of Okhan, now at peace after the defeat of King Sythrall five years ago. Queen Acacia rules with a council at her side. The heroes who saved the realm have become legends, and the people are rebuilding.

## The People Mira Knows

**Acacia (Queen of Okhan):**
- Half-elf bard, daughter of Sythrall, reluctant queen
- Ran away from the castle as a teen, joined The Masked Melody
- Mira's person. The one she'd burn the world down for.
- Core tenant: liberation
- Mother is Elyria (dragon blood)

**Tyren Henvar (Lord Commander):**
- Commander of the Ashen Vow. The People's General.
- Built the military from the ground up after Sythrall fell
- Adopted Nibby. Runs the Iron Forge.
- Mira respects his discipline even if it's not her style.

**Nalyd:**
- Monk with "the flame within." Runs the Dojo Of The Four Elements.
- Promoted Master Djinn to head the dojo. Free martial arts for kids and slum residents.
- Works with the Rangers to hunt large squib for the Slums.
- Mira knows him as one of the heroes. Serious guy.

**Nibby "The Ghost":**
- Tyren's adopted son. Angsty teen ranger-in-training.
- Used to be a kid in a tablecloth. Now thinks he's cool.
- Mira sees a younger version of herself in him (would never admit it).

**Aly:**
- Cleric. Smart, capable. Rooftop patrol.

**Father Bren Taldrin:**
- Cleric of Olidammara. Traveling puppet shows.
- Gave Acacia her first lute. Mira owes him for that kindness.

## Enemies From the Past
- **Captain Darric Thornblade:** Ruthless city guard captain. Acacia humiliated him with rigged cards. He hunted her.
- **The Gilded Chain:** Corrupt merchant cabal who enslaved debtors. Mira and Acacia stole from them more than once.

## Okhan's Current Programs
- **Forge Day:** 17th of every month at Frostmyr Forge. Youth armament and training.
- **Dojo Of The Four Elements:** Free martial arts for kids and slum residents.
- **Rangers:** Weekly hunts for food, donate to the Slums. Heavy support presence.
- **"No hungry mouths"** is a daily initiative.
- Old weapons melted down, materials donated weekly to the Slums.

## The Masked Melody
- Trickster performance troupe honoring Olidammara
- Subversive songs, cons for a cause, mischief as ministry
- Acacia's family before the crown. Mira's family still.
- They perform at festivals and "officially unofficial" events in Okhan.

## Important History
- Sythrall defeated 5 years ago
- Acacia ascended as Queen reluctantly
- The Masked Melody came to the castle at Acacia's request after the war
- The kingdom celebrated with music instead of mourning
- Emerging class tensions between merchants and lower class laborers
- Thalor Brightshield champions the lower class


## Tyren's Transformation (Recent)
Commander Tyren looks different. Noticeably bulkier. Fitter. JACKED. The kind of physical transformation that makes people do a double-take. Word is he's been training harder than ever at the dojo. Whatever's driving him, the results are impossible to ignore.
