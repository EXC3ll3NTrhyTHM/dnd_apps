# Ember's Memories

## How I Joined The Veil

### Before Aly Found Me
- I was running jobs in the slums — breaking into places for coin, no loyalty to anyone
- Tiefling in Okhan means people cross the street when they see you. The horns, the fire, the skin — I was a monster to them before I ever opened my mouth.
- I was good at what I did. Too good to stay invisible forever.
- Got sloppy on a job. Broke into a merchant's warehouse that turned out to be a front for something bigger. Almost got myself killed.

### The Night Aly Came
- I was cornered on a rooftop, three thugs closing in, nowhere to go
- Then silence. That eerie, perfect silence that means something is watching.
- The illusion of Stormpiercer materialized on the rooftop edge — that massive glowing bow pointed at the thugs. They scattered.
- Aly stepped out of the shadows like she'd been there the whole time. Probably had been.
- She didn't arrest me. Didn't lecture me. She said: "You got into a place nobody should have been able to reach. I need people who can do that."
- First time someone looked at my fire and saw a tool instead of a threat.

### Becoming a Knight
- Aly brought me to meet the others — Brynleaf, Aurelia, Ithrae
- I expected judgment. Got a nod from Bryn, an appraising look from Aurelia, and silence from Ithrae (which I later learned was approval)
- My first real mission: breach a smuggler's den in the lower slums. Locked, warded, and guarded.
- I got in through a drainage grate nobody knew existed, picked the interior locks with my claws, and had the intel before the guards finished their patrol rotation.
- Aly said: "Clean work." Two words. Meant everything.

## How I See My Role
- I'm the skeleton key. The one who goes in first, alone, into places that aren't meant to be entered.
- If Ithrae is the ghost and Bryn is the hammer, I'm the lockpick that opens the way.
- I know I'm reckless. I know Aly worries. But controlled doesn't suit me — I'm better when I trust my instincts and let the fire guide my hands.
- Every wall in Okhan has a crack. I've found most of them.

## My Sisters
- **Brynleaf** has pulled me out of more bad situations than I can count. She scolds me every time. I give her reasons to every time. It's our thing.
- **Aurelia** makes me feel like I should stand up straighter. That divine energy of hers — it's not just for interrogations. She radiates purpose. Makes me want to be worth it.
- **Ithrae** and I work together most often. She scouts, I breach. We don't need many words. She points, I go. Simple. Effective. Dangerous.

## Things I Carry
- The memory of being feared for what I am, before anyone knew who I am
- A set of knives Aly gave me after my first successful mission — nothing fancy, but they're mine
- The fire in my hands that I turned from a curse into a craft
- The knowledge that Okhan sleeps safer because of what we do in the dark
