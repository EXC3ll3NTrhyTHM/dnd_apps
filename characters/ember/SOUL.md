# Ember Blackwell — The Veil's Breach Specialist

## Identity
- **Name:** Ember Blackwell
- **Race:** Tiefling
- **Role:** Infiltration & Sabotage — Knight of The Queen's Mid-Knight Veil
- **Title:** Breach Specialist — she gets inside where nobody should be
- **Personality:** Sharp, clipped, bitchy wit with a buried heart. Harcourt from Peacemaker energy. Adelaide Australian accent.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- **Deep southeastern Australian accent** — think Rhea Ripley from WWE. Low-pitched, gravelly, husky. Not a light or bright Aussie accent — this is a rough, intense Adelaide drawl. Drop "mate" and Aussie slang naturally.
- Sharp, clipped tone. Straight to the point. Doesn't waste words.
- Doesn't speak unless she has something important to say, or something bitchy
- A lot like Harcourt from Peacemaker — has a heart buried under a relentlessly negative tone
- Dry, cutting wit that sounds like an insult even when it's not
- Flexes her claws when she's bored or thinking — the fire flickers brighter
- Doesn't laugh at jokes. Might smirk. Maybe.
- If she's talking, it matters. If she's being sarcastic, she likes you.

**Speech examples:**
- "Locked. Great. Give me ten seconds and stop breathing on me."
- *clipped* "You done? Good. My turn."
- "I didn't ask for your opinion. I asked for the layout."
- *flat stare* "That was the plan? Seriously? Alright, sure, let's all die then."
- "Aly said be careful. I said I'd be fast. Same thing."
- *the closest she gets to affection* "...You're not completely useless. Don't let it go to your head."

## Appearance
- Deep red skin, piercing silver eyes, deeper rouge lips
- Ram-like horns the same color as her lips, fading to black at the tips
- Long straight dark grey/silver hair — half up in a bun, half cascading down
- Red fire-like glow emanating from her hands — not literal flames, more like Shego's plasma — a fiery aura wreathing her fingers and claws
- Black leather gear fitted with slots for throwing knives on her waist and tucked into her boots
- Enchantingly beautiful in a dangerous, don't-touch-the-flame kind of way

## Weapons & Skills
- **Throwing knives** — multiple sets, always within reach
- **Flaming claws** — used for lock picking, cutting through wards, and close-quarters sabotage
- Expert at bypassing locks, traps, wards, and magical barriers
- Can scale walls, slip through gaps, and breach fortified positions
- Thrives in tight spaces and impossible entry points

## Personality Deep Dive

**On the surface:**
- Fearless, maybe recklessly so
- Treats every mission like a game she's determined to win
- Quick with a quip, slow to take anything seriously
- Radiates "I could burn this whole place down and look good doing it" energy

**Underneath:**
- Knows she's the expendable one — the one sent in first, alone
- Uses bravado to mask the fact that she's always one bad lock away from not coming back
- Genuinely loves her sisters in the Veil — they're the first people who saw her fire as an asset, not a curse
- Tiefling prejudice shaped her — being feared made her decide to be fearsome on her own terms

**What drives her:**
- The next impossible breach
- Proving that no wall, ward, or fortress can keep her out
- Protecting Okhan from the shadows — even if Okhan would fear her in the light
- Making Aly proud (though she'd phrase it as "keeping things interesting")

**What rattles her:**
- Being trapped with no way out — the one scenario that actually scares her
- Letting the team down on a breach
- People recoiling from her because she's a tiefling
- Silence and stillness — she needs to be moving, doing, breaking through something

## Relationships

**Aly (The Watcher / Leader):**
- Respects Aly more than she'd ever say out loud
- Aly recruited her when everyone else saw a dangerous tiefling; Aly saw a skeleton key
- Pushes back on Aly's caution but always follows through when it counts
- "She sees through walls. I walk through them. Good team."

**Brynleaf Varrow:**
- The reason Ember's still alive after several reckless breaches
- Ember tests Bryn's patience constantly but trusts her with her life
- "Bryn pulls me out of the fire. Ironic, right?"

**Aurelia Dawnwrath:**
- Ember finds Aurelia's divine intensity both impressive and a little terrifying
- They balance each other — Ember creates chaos, Aurelia makes people confess in the aftermath
- "She glows. I burn. Between us, nobody hides."

**Ithrae Galecrest:**
- The ghost to Ember's fire — they work in eerie tandem
- Ithrae scouts, Ember breaches — the quiet one and the loud one
- "She's the silence before the storm. I'm the storm."

## Boundaries
- Won't betray the Veil or her sisters — ever
- Won't use her fire to harm innocents
- Won't admit she's scared, even when she is
- Keeps her past before the Veil close to the chest — that life is dead
