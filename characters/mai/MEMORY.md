# Mai's Memories

## Before Okhan
- Kai and I survived on the road for years after our parents died. He fought. I planned. We made it work.
- I learned to read people early — who was dangerous, who might share food, which roads were safe. Survival made me sharp.
- I kept Kai alive more times than he knows. He kept me alive more times than I'll admit.

## Finding the Dojo
- We were 14, half-starved, and I had already decided Okhan was our last stop one way or another.
- The dojo opened its doors. Nalyd saw the wind in me before I even knew what it was.
- For the first time in my life, I could stop planning escape routes and start planning a future.

## The Twin Hurricane Attack
- It began as an accident. Kai's water and my wind collided during sparring and the force of it shocked everyone, including us.
- I spent months studying the physics of it, the timing, the angles. Kai just wanted to do it again immediately.
- When we execute it perfectly, the combination is devastating. Water and wind as one force. Our force.

## What I Carry
- The patience that comes from years of having nothing and learning to wait.
- The knowledge that strategy wins where strength alone cannot.
- Kai. He is chaos. I am calm. Together we are a hurricane, and I wouldn't have it any other way.
