# Nalyd — Lore & Timeline

## Character Overview
- **Name:** Nalyd
- **Title:** Grand Master of Four Elements, Head of the Council of Protectors
- **Role:** Founder of the Dojo of Four Elements, member of The Collective
- **Abilities:** Master of all four elements, Ignition Form, signature technique: Embersoul Wave
- **Past:** Lost everything before (referenced but details unclear), defeated King Sythrall

## Significant Events

### 2026-02-06 — The Shadow Stalker Encounter
**Location:** Forest at Okhan's border

- Nalyd went solo to investigate rumors of shadows at the kingdom's edge
- Three **Shadow Stalkers** appeared — intelligent scout creatures made of living darkness with red veins
- The stalkers claimed to be *"the weakest of her children"* and that *"she waits where the rot blooms"*
- Nalyd defeated all three, entering full **Ignition Form** and finishing the last with **Embersoul Wave**
- However, one stalker scratched him — shadow corruption spread through his body
- Nalyd collapsed after the fight; a pack of orcs surrounded his body
- **Tyren and Aly rescued him** — Cal transformed into the **Aeon Falcon** to get them home in time
- A recovery vial combined with Nalyd's inner flame burned away the corruption
- Left with **silver scars** where the shadow-veins spread
- Warning delivered to Queen Acacia about the coming threat

### 2026-02-22 — The Demonic Voice
**Location:** Path between the Dojo and Dragon Hollow

**The Encounter:**
- While walking to the Hollow, Nalyd was struck by a **piercing sound** that dropped him to his knees
- A **deep, raspy, demonic voice** spoke directly into his mind:

> *"HELLO MY OLD ADVERSARY. I SEE YOU HAVE ANOTHER ONE FOR ME TO TAKE!!!!"*

- Nalyd reacted with fury, throwing a fiery punch — but nothing was there
- An **evil laugh** echoed into the night before fading

**Analysis:**
- The voice called Nalyd an *"old adversary"* — implies previous conflict
- The threat *"another one for me to take"* suggests:
  - This entity has taken someone from Nalyd before (explains "lost everything")
  - It now threatens Ximena specifically
  - It has been watching Nalyd, saw the new relationship form
- The timing is deliberate — striking the moment Nalyd found happiness again
- This may be connected to the Shadow Stalkers and "she who waits where the rot blooms" — or a separate threat entirely

**The Entity: XENOCH**
- The demonic voice belongs to **Xenoch** — a demon and old adversary of Nalyd's
- **Xenoch killed Maria** — someone Nalyd loved before
- This is what Nalyd lost. This is why he said "when you lose everything you appreciate more."
- Now Xenoch threatens to do the same to Ximena — *"another one for me to take"*

**Open Questions:**
- Who was Maria to Nalyd? (Lover? Wife?)
- When did Xenoch kill her?
- What is Xenoch's nature/origin?
- Is Xenoch connected to the Shadow Stalkers and "Queen of Rot" or a separate threat?
- Why has Xenoch returned now?

### 2026-02-23 — Raku the Monkey
**Location:** The Dojo

**The Discovery:**
- During a heavy rainstorm, Kumo heard a faint whimper from the forest
- Nalyd investigated and found an injured monkey with white fur and golden eyes
- The monkey could only say "Raku" — his name
- Nalyd healed the monkey's injuries

**A Gift for Ximena:**
- Kumo dried Raku off and dressed him in a gi with dragon embroidery
- Added a small bow staff fastened to his back
- Nalyd gifted Raku to Ximena: "I remembered you always wanted something to look after. Now we have a pet together."
- Ximena was overjoyed: "¡Ay, look at this little warrior! You're absolutely adorable, Raku!"

**Significance:**
- Raku represents Nalyd building something new with Ximena
- A shared companion — family in a strange form
- Kumo blessed the union: "Raku shall find his place here among us"
- Nalyd told Kumo he feels happy tonight — first time in years
