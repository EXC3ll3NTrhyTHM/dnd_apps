# Xenoch — Antagonist

## Overview
- **Name:** Xenoch
- **Type:** Demonic entity
- **Status:** Active threat
- **Primary Target:** Nalyd

---

## Known Information

### Voice & Presence
- Deep, raspy, demonic voice
- Can project voice directly into a target's mind
- Causes piercing pain/sound when making contact
- Laughs with malevolent amusement
- Can observe from a distance without physical presence

### History with Nalyd
- Calls Nalyd *"my old adversary"* — they have fought before
- **Killed Maria** — someone Nalyd loved
- This is what Nalyd lost. The reason he carries so much weight.
- *"Another one for me to take"* = Xenoch now threatens Ximena
- Pattern: Xenoch targets those Nalyd loves to torment him

---

## Timeline

### 2026-02-22 — First Appearance (Current Campaign)
**Location:** Path between the Dojo and Dragon Hollow

- Struck Nalyd with a piercing sound, dropping him to his knees
- Spoke directly into his mind:
  > *"HELLO MY OLD ADVERSARY. I SEE YOU HAVE ANOTHER ONE FOR ME TO TAKE!!!!"*
- Nalyd attacked with a fiery punch but hit nothing
- Xenoch laughed and vanished
- **Context:** This occurred moments after Nalyd left Ximena's quarters, having just found happiness with her for the first time in years
- **Implication:** Xenoch has been watching. Knows about Ximena. Timing was deliberate.

### 2026-02-24 — Ongoing Contact
**Location:** Dragon's Hollow & The Dojo

- Nalyd told Bonesy: *"Something evil has been contacting me."*
- Asked if Bonesy could see if something was haunting him
- Said he tried to block/banish it but *"it didn't take"*
- **Xenoch is persistent** — standard cleansing methods ineffective

**Ximena Encounter:**
- While keeping watch over sleeping Nalyd at the dojo, **Ximena heard a faint evil laugh**
- She wasn't certain if it was real, but went on high alert
- **Implication:** Xenoch may now be targeting/observing Ximena directly, not just through Nalyd
