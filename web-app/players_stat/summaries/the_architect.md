## Backstory

You were born under a rare comet — a celestial omen said to mark those touched by Bahamut himself. Your parents were humble smiths, but from a young age, you were different: your pale blue eyes glowed in the dark, and your presence stirred awe or unease wherever you walked.

At age 10, you were found by Sir Alric Dawnvale, a wandering paladin of the Order of the Platinum Scale. He took you to the Sanctum of the Silver Flame, where you were raised not only in combat but in compassion, discipline, and divine justice. You trained beside many, but none more closely than Darian Vex — a fellow orphan and your closest companion.

But where your heart burned for justice, Darian’s burned for power. He grew bitter, frustrated with the Order’s reluctance to wield its strength ruthlessly. When he betrayed the Order and joined the cult known as the Hollow Brand, you were sent to bring him back. Instead, you found him leading the burning of a peaceful village — and he warned you that mercy would be your undoing.

You hesitated, and he escaped.

Now, you walk the world as a knight of Bahamut — a beacon of hope and judgment. You have vowed to protect the innocent, bring light to shadowed lands, and stop Darian before he helps Tiamat’s faithful rise in flame and tyranny.

You bear a holy symbol etched into a silver scale, a gift from Sir Alric. You still wear it daily — not just to honor Bahamut, but to remind yourself why you fight.
