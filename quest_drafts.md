# Quest Drafts - Tyren (General Quests)

These are draft quests designed for Tyren to go on one-on-one with each of his three generals. Each quest introduces a piece of the fungal rot arc through the lens of that general's branch. No new NPCs needed beyond the generals themselves (and one missing officer). No combat. Slice-of-life investigation with escalating unease.

---

## 🛡️ JONAH'S QUEST: "The Missing Watch"

**Companions:** Jonah Varrow (The Okhan Shield)
**Location:** Lower residential district, old storage cellars near the docks
**Reward:** Gold + Intel

### How Jonah Brings It Up

Jonah catches Tyren during a routine patrol check-in. He's not panicked, but he's clearly bothered. One of his best officers, a guy named Harlen, hasn't shown up for two shifts. Harlen isn't the type to skip. Jonah already sent someone to his home... door was open, bed hadn't been slept in. Jonah doesn't want to make it official yet because it'll spook the other Shield officers. He asks Tyren to come with him personally to retrace Harlen's last patrol route. "I'd rather it be us than a search party."

**Conversation Flow:**
- Jonah opens serious, not casual. Pulls Tyren aside, not in front of the other officers.
- If Tyren asks questions: Harlen's route was the lower residential district near the docks. Last seen two nights ago. Nobody saw anything.
- If Tyren suggests sending a squad: Jonah pushes back - "Not yet. Could be nothing. Could be he's on a bender. But if it's something else, I don't want rumors before we know."
- If Tyren agrees: Jonah's relieved. He's already got Harlen's route mapped.
- If Tyren declines or delays: Jonah respects it but says he's going anyway. Leaves the door open.

---

### Stage 1: "Harlen's Route"

Tyren and Jonah walk Harlen's patrol path through the lower residential district. Night shift streets. Quiet. Jonah points out the checkpoints, the doorways Harlen would have ducked into, the alleys he'd scan. Everything looks normal.

**Actions:**
- 🔍 **Check the alley corners** - Nothing obvious, but one alley has a faint smell. Damp, earthy. Not sewer smell. Different.
- 💬 **Ask Jonah about Harlen** - Jonah says Harlen was steady, loyal, never dramatic. Ten years on the Shield. If he disappeared, something happened TO him.
- 🥾 **Continue the route** *(advances)* - They reach the end of Harlen's normal path. But there are bootprints continuing past his usual turnaround, heading toward an old storage district.

---

### Stage 2: "Off the Path"

The bootprints lead to a section of old cellars and storage rooms near the docks. Some haven't been used in years. Jonah didn't know Harlen had any reason to come down here.

**Actions:**
- 🔍 **Examine the bootprints** - They're Harlen's, standard Shield-issue boots. But the stride gets weird further in. Shorter steps, like he was being cautious or confused.
- 👃 **Follow the smell** - That damp earthy scent is stronger here. Coming from below. One of the cellar doors is slightly ajar.
- 💬 **Ask Jonah if this area is patrolled** - Jonah says no, it's been off the rotation for months. Nobody down here but rats.
- 🚪 **Open the cellar** *(advances)* - They descend.

---

### Stage 3: "The Cellar"

They descend into the cellar. It's dark, cold, and the smell is thick. The walls are stone, slick with moisture. But deeper in, the moisture isn't water. There's a faint film on the walls, slightly greenish in the torchlight. Organic. Soft to the touch. And at the far end of the cellar, slumped against the wall: Harlen.

**Actions:**
- 🕯️ **Examine the walls** - The film is thin, almost like moss, but it's not moss. It's spongy. Warm. Pulling a piece away reveals tendrils underneath clinging to the stone. It's growing.
- 💬 **Talk to Harlen** - He's alive but foggy. Says he heard something during patrol, came down to check. Then... he's not sure. He sat down to rest "for a minute." He thinks it's been a few hours. It's been two days. He looks okay physically but his skin around the neck has a faint grayish-green tinge.
- 🤚 **Check Harlen's condition** - He's cold, clammy, pupils are slow. Not injured. Not poisoned in any way they recognize. Just... off. Like he was somewhere else and only part of him came back.
- 🚶 **Get Harlen out** *(advances)* - They pull him out of the cellar.

---

### Stage 4: "The Decision"

They're back above ground. Harlen is lucid enough to walk but keeps saying he's fine, he just fell asleep. Jonah looks at Tyren. This is Tyren's call now.

**Key Decisions:**
- 🏥 **Send Harlen to the healers, seal the cellar** - The safe play. Harlen gets checked out, the cellar gets locked. Jonah posts a guard. But whatever's growing down there stays undisturbed.
- 🔬 **Take a sample of the growth, investigate further** - Tyren scrapes some of the wall film into a container. Could give it to Aly for study, or bring it to the council. But now they're carrying it.
- 🔥 **Burn the cellar** - Aggressive response. Torch whatever's growing down there. Satisfying but they lose any chance to study it, and the fire might not reach everything.
- 🤫 **Keep it quiet for now** - Don't report it officially. Just Tyren and Jonah know. Monitor Harlen. Watch the area. Less panic, but less help too.
- 📋 **Full report to the council** - Go official. Alert all branches. Reah's Rangers and Cena's fleet get informed. Maximum response but word spreads fast.

**Aftermath (regardless of choice):**
Harlen "recovers" within a day or two. But he develops a persistent cough. And he doesn't sleep well anymore. Says the dreams are about roots.

### Full Report Implications

**Who hears it:** The council - Tyren, Jonah, Reah, Cena, and any other leadership.

**What they'd learn:**
- A Shield officer went missing for two days in a cellar beneath the lower residential district
- He has no memory of the time lost
- There's an unknown organic growth on the cellar walls - soft, warm, alive
- Harlen is physically fine but "off" - discolored skin, slow pupils, claims he was only gone minutes

**What they would NOT know:**
- What the growth is
- Where it came from
- Whether it's dangerous
- Whether it's spreading
- Whether Harlen is actually fine

**The good:** All three branches are now on alert. More eyes, faster detection.

**The bad:** Word spreads. Council members talk to lieutenants, lieutenants to soldiers, soldiers to families. Within a week, rumors circulate: "Something's growing under the city." People avoid the lower district. Merchants reroute. Dockworkers get nervous. Could be panic over what might be nothing.

**The real tension:** If Tyren goes full report now, it's early. One missing officer and some weird moss. Might look like an overreaction. Jonah might push back since he came to Tyren quietly for a reason. But if Tyren keeps it quiet and it turns out to be the first sign of something massive... he sat on it.

## 🏹 REAH'S QUEST: "The Dead Trail"

**Companions:** Reah (Army Ranger Corps)
**Location:** Eastern forest patrol route
**Reward:** Gold + Intel

### How Reah Brings It Up

Reah doesn't come to Tyren through channels. She shows up at The Barracks, unannounced, drops her field pack on the table and says "I need you to come see something." No preamble, no report. This is personal. Her Rangers found a section of their patrol route where the forest went wrong. She's been out there twice now and she doesn't like it. She's not scared, she's pissed. Something's messing with her woods and she wants Tyren's eyes on it before she decides what to do.

**Conversation Flow:**
- Reah opens blunt. No small talk. "Something's wrong out on the eastern route. I need you to see it yourself."
- If Tyren asks what's wrong: "I'll show you. I don't want to put words on it and have you show up expecting something it's not. You need to see it cold."
- If Tyren asks if it's dangerous: She scoffs. "If it was dangerous I'd have handled it. It's... I don't know what it is. That's the problem."
- If Tyren suggests sending Rangers: "My Rangers already found it. I need someone who thinks bigger than a patrol report."
- If Tyren agrees: She's ready to go immediately. "Grab your boots."
- If Tyren pushes back: Reah gets quiet. Then: "Tyren. I wouldn't ask if it wasn't important." That's as close to vulnerable as she gets.

---

### Stage 1: "The Ranger Path"

Reah leads Tyren out along the eastern patrol route. Deep forest. Reah moves fast and confident. She knows every tree, every rock. She's talking about her Rangers on the way - training, supply complaints, normal stuff. Then she stops talking.

**Actions:**
- 💬 **Ask about her Rangers** - She's proud but won't say it outright. Talks about two recruits who are "not completely useless yet." Classic Reah.
- 🌲 **Take in the forest** - Beautiful. Dense canopy, bird calls, animal tracks. Healthy forest. Makes what's coming feel worse.
- 👂 **Notice the change** - At some point, Tyren realizes the birds stopped. He can't pinpoint when.
- 🥾 **Follow Reah** *(advances)* - She's picked up the pace. Her jaw is set. They're getting close.

---

### Stage 2: "The Silent Stretch"

They cross a line. No marker, no visible change, but suddenly there is nothing. No birds. No insects. No wind through the leaves. The trees look fine. The canopy is full. But the undergrowth is different: thicker, darker green, and everything is leaning the same direction. Toward the center of this zone. Reah stops. "This is it."

**Actions:**
- 🔍 **Examine the undergrowth** - The plants are healthy. Too healthy. Lush and green in a way that doesn't match the season. Leaves slightly waxy. Stems thicker than normal. All oriented the same way, like pointing at something.
- 👂 **Listen carefully** - Silence. But not empty. A low, almost sub-audible hum. Felt more than heard. Coming from the ground.
- 💬 **Ask Reah what she thinks** - "I think the forest is sick. Not dying. The opposite. Something's feeding it and it's growing wrong." She kicks at the undergrowth. It springs back immediately.
- 🥾 **Push toward the center** *(advances)* - Deeper into the zone.

---

### Stage 3: "The Soft Ground"

At the center of the dead zone, there's a clearing. Not natural. The trees thinned out here like they were pushed back. The ground is covered in thick moss-like growth, deep green, almost black in places. And it's warm. Noticeably warm. Stepping on it is like stepping on something alive. It gives under their weight.

**Actions:**
- 🤚 **Touch the ground** - Warm. Soft. Almost like skin. Moisture beading on the surface. Not dew. Coming from inside, like the ground is sweating.
- 🔍 **Dig into it** - Under the moss layer, about an inch down, pale tendrils running through the soil. Thread-thin, white, branching in every direction. A network. A root system of something that isn't a tree.
- 💬 **Ask Reah her recommendation** - "Burn it. All of it. Salt the earth. I don't know what this is but it doesn't belong here." She's dead serious.
- 🌡️ **Check the temperature** - The center is warmer than the edges. Something below is generating heat. Alive.

*(Advances to final stage)*

---

### Stage 4: "The Call"

They've seen enough. Time to decide.

**Key Decisions:**
- 🔥 **Burn it, like Reah says** - Rangers torch the whole stretch. Satisfying, immediate. But they don't know if it'll work or if whatever's below will just grow back. Smoke might carry spores.
- 🚧 **Quarantine the area** - Mark it off, reroute patrols, keep people away. Monitor it. See if it spreads. Slow and cautious.
- 🔬 **Collect samples, bring them to the council** - Study it first. Reah hates this: "You want to bring pieces of that thing INTO the city?"
- 🗺️ **Map the boundary** - Figure out exactly how big it is before doing anything. Reah likes this one better, at least it's tactical. Rangers mark the edges and check weekly for growth.
- 📋 **Loop in Aly's network** - Have the Shadows watch the area from a distance. See if anyone or anything has been visiting the zone. Good intel play.

**Aftermath (regardless of choice):**
Reah marks the location personally. Two weeks later, her Rangers report the boundary has expanded twelve feet in every direction.

## ⚓ CENA'S QUEST: "The Drifting Catch"

**Companions:** Cena Marella (Royal Naval Fleet)
**Location:** Dry Dock 3, Okhan harbor
**Reward:** Gold + Intel

### How Cena Brings It Up

Cena sends a falcon to Tyren. Short message, very Cena: "Come to Dry Dock 3 when you can. Got something weird. Bring your curiosity, leave your appetite." When Tyren arrives, Cena's standing with his arms crossed, looking at the hull of a supply ship that's been pulled up for inspection. He's got that half-smile he puts on when something bothers him but he doesn't want the sailors to see. "So... you know how I said the coastal routes have been smooth lately? I may have spoken too soon."

**Conversation Flow:**
- Cena's casual at first. Doesn't want to make it a big deal. "Probably nothing, but I wanted a second opinion before I file anything."
- If Tyren asks what it is: Cena walks him over to the hull. "Easier to show you."
- If Tyren asks if the crew is okay: Cena nods. "Ship's fine, crew's fine. It's what hitched a ride that's got me thinking."
- If Tyren suggests fleet channels: Cena hesitates. "I could. But my guys are already rattled. Last thing I need is an official inspection before I know what we're looking at."
- If Tyren agrees: Cena's grateful but plays it cool. "Knew I could count on you."
- If Tyren's busy: Cena doesn't push hard but mentions "The thing is still... doing something. I'd feel better if it was sooner."

---

### Stage 1: "The Dry Dock"

Tyren arrives at Dry Dock 3. The supply ship *Greenwater's Pride* is up on blocks. Cena's cleared out the workers, just a couple trusted sailors left. The dock smells like salt and tar, normal harbor stuff. Cena leads Tyren underneath the hull to the starboard side.

**Actions:**
- 💬 **Ask the sailors** - They're uneasy. Noticed the ship pulling slightly to starboard on the return trip. Thought it was drag from barnacles. It wasn't.
- 🔍 **Inspect the hull** - Normal wear, some barnacles, seaweed. Then about two-thirds back: a mass clinging to the hull. About the size of a shield. Organic. Not barnacle, not coral, not seaweed.
- 👃 **Smell** - Sweet. Not pleasant-sweet. Rot-sweet. Like overripe fruit left in a warm room.
- 🥾 **Get closer** *(advances)* - Time to examine the growth.

---

### Stage 2: "The Growth"

Up close, the mass is unsettling. Textured like coral but soft to the touch, almost spongy. Pale green-grey with darker veins running through it. Attached to the hull by thin tendrils that burrowed into the wood between the tar seams. And it's pulsing. Slowly. Rhythmically. Like breathing.

**Actions:**
- 🤚 **Touch it** - Warm. Slightly damp. Surface gives under pressure and slowly rebounds. When pressed, the veins darken momentarily, like blood moving. Doesn't react aggressively. Just... is.
- 🔪 **Cut a piece off** - Separates easily. Cut surface weeps a clear fluid. The piece keeps pulsing for about a minute after separation, then goes still. Cena watches with visible discomfort.
- 💬 **Ask Cena about the route** - Standard coastal supply run. Port Avall to Okhan, three days. Nothing unusual except... Cena pulls out a chart. The helmsman noted cloudy water about a half-day out from harbor, in a cove they usually pass through. Stayed in it for about an hour.
- 🗺️ **Check the chart** *(advances)* - The cloudy water was in Kettle Cove, a sheltered inlet on the coast. Not on any regular patrol route.

---

### Stage 3: "The Source Question"

They've seen the growth. They know roughly where it came from. Now what?

**Actions:**
- 💬 **Ask Cena about other ships** - Two other ships passed through the same cove recently. One hasn't been inspected yet. The other was scraped clean last week - dock workers mentioned "stubborn barnacles" harder to remove than usual. Nobody thought anything of it.
- 🔍 **Examine the tendril roots** - Penetrated about half an inch into the hull wood. The wood around them is slightly discolored, softer. The growth is digesting it. Slowly.
- 💬 **Ask Cena what his gut says** - "My gut says this thing is alive, it's eating my ship, and there's more of it where it came from." He's not joking anymore.
- ⚓ *(advances to decision)*

---

### Stage 4: "Tyren's Call"

Cena looks to Tyren. His sailors are watching from a distance.

**Key Decisions:**
- 🔥 **Scrape and burn it** - Remove the growth, torch it, re-tar the hull. Clean and simple. But whatever's in Kettle Cove is still there.
- 🚢 **Inspect all ships that passed through the cove** - Full fleet inspection. Disruptive and expensive. Cena doesn't love it but won't fight it. Could reveal how widespread this is.
- ⛵ **Send a scouting vessel to Kettle Cove** - Go see what's in that water. Could be small, could be massive. Risk is sending more ships into potentially contaminated waters.
- 🔬 **Keep the sample, study it** - Preserve the cut piece for analysis. Give to Aly or someone with arcane knowledge. Cena will seal it in a jar. "If that thing starts growing in a jar in my office, I'm blaming you."
- 🚫 **Quarantine the cove** - Mark it off-limits to all naval traffic until further notice. Safe play but cuts off a useful shortcut route. Adds a day to supply runs.

**Aftermath (regardless of choice):**
Cena orders the other uninspected ship pulled into dry dock. They find a smaller growth on the keel. It's been there longer. The wood underneath is soft and spongy, almost like the growth is slowly replacing it.

## 🔗 The Connected Thread

None of these quests reference each other directly. But if Tyren does all three:

- **Jonah** found it underground, in the city, affecting a person
- **Reah** found it in the forest, affecting the land
- **Cena** found it in the water, affecting ships

It's everywhere. Under the streets, in the woods, in the sea. Whatever this is, it's not isolated. It's a network. And it's been growing quietly for a while.

The moment Tyren connects the dots (or someone at the council table does), that's when Bound By Rot truly begins.

## 🍺 Original Drunk Encounter (Standalone Patrol Scene)

This was the original seed idea for the fungal thread. Can be used as a standalone patrol moment or as foreshadowing before the general quests.

**Setup:** Tyren's on his M/W/F patrol with The Shield, routine night. He finds a man slumped against a wall, clearly wasted, but something's off.

**Details:**
- Skin has a faint grayish-green tinge around the neck and behind the ears, easy to miss in torchlight
- Eyes are glassy but not bloodshot - cloudy, like pond water
- Mumbling rhythmically, almost like reciting something he doesn't understand
- Skin is cold and slightly damp when touched - not sweaty. Cellar damp.
- Doesn't react to pain normally

**What the drunk says (if questioned):**
- The ground was "breathing"
- He went into a cellar looking for a place to sleep and "the walls were soft"
- Can't say where the cellar was

**Resolution:** He "sobers up" by morning, doesn't remember much. Develops a persistent cough. Doesn't sleep well. Dreams about roots.

This encounter can happen before or after any of the general quests. It's the civilian version of the same phenomenon.
