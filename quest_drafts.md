# Quest Drafts — Nalyd / Dojo Quests

All focused on discovery and hard choices, not combat. Companion is a dojo warrior.

---

## 1. "The Hands That Were" (Companion: Kumo)

A traveling blacksmith arrives in Okhan claiming he can identify the ancient craft that made Kumo's metal hands, and that their creator is still alive. Kumo won't go alone. Nalyd and Kumo journey to a remote mountain forge, only to discover the truth about how Kumo lost his hands and why the craftsman made them. The hard choice: Kumo can have his real hands restored through an ancient ritual, but the metal hands hold a power Okhan might need someday. Does Kumo choose his humanity or his duty?

**Status:** Draft

---

## 2. "What Burns Beneath" (Companion: Edgar)

During a routine earth training session, Edgar senses something deep underground, something alive. He and Nalyd descend into forgotten caverns beneath Okhan and discover an ancient sealed chamber containing something from before the kingdom. The deeper they go, the more Edgar opens up about what he lost and why he chose to bury himself in duty. The hard choice: what they find could change Okhan forever, but the knowledge comes with a cost, and Edgar has to decide if some things are better left buried.

**Status:** Draft

---

## 3. "The School She Left Behind" (Companion: Ximena)

A letter arrives for Ximena. Her old martial arts school is in crisis, her former students scattered, the legacy crumbling. She won't admit she cares, but Nalyd sees through the bravado. They travel to her homeland together. The hard choice: Ximena can save her old school, but only by leaving the dojo and returning permanently. Or she can let it die and accept that some chapters end. Nalyd has to guide his rival through the hardest decision of her life without making it for her.

**Status:** Draft

---

## 4. "Still Waters" (Companion: Kai & Mai)

The twins start losing sync on the Twin Hurricane Attack. Something's wrong, but neither will talk about it. Nalyd takes them on a journey to a sacred elemental spring said to reveal inner truth. Along the way, the twins' unspoken tension boils over: Mai is tired of always being the responsible one, and Kai is afraid he's holding her back. The hard choice: the spring reveals that the twins' power would actually be greater if they trained apart. Do they break the bond that defines them to become individually stronger?

**Status:** Draft

---

## 5. "The Man at the Gate" (Companion: Djinn)

A stranger arrives at the dojo claiming to be Djinn's father. Djinn, who says "that person no longer exists" about his life before the dojo, refuses to see him. But the man won't leave, and his story starts making waves among the students. Nalyd must decide how to handle this without overstepping, while Djinn faces the past he's been running from. The hard choice: the father is dying and wants forgiveness. Does Djinn open that door, or does the Head of the Dojo stay behind the wall he built?

**Status:** In Development
