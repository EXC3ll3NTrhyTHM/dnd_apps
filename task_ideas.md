# Nalyd

Based on Dylan's character description.

---

## Daily Duties

### Giant Squid Hunting
- Nalyd fights giant squids as training + delivers them as food to the slums and residential areas
- **Rewards:** XP from the fight, reputation with slums/residential folks
- Two-for-one: combat training and community service

### Solo Training / Breakthrough Work
- Rigorous personal training, working on new techniques
- Pushing toward his next power level ("on the cusp of something beyond")
- **Rewards:** XP with chance at milestone unlocks over time

### Council Leadership
- Head of the Council of Protectors — makes decisions to keep up protection of Okhan
- **Rewards:** Stipend gold, access to protector intel and missions

---

## Weekly Duties

### Gladiatorial Games
- Fights in the arena for extra training
- **Rewards:** Big XP, gold from winnings/bets, chance at rare items or recognition
- Natural scaling as he pushes for next level

### Dojo Sessions (Biweekly)
- Checking in on Master Djinn's transition to running the dojo full-time
- Sparring with top students
- **Rewards:** Small XP, maintaining dojo connection
- Nalyd only there a couple times every 2 weeks now

### Protector Dispatch
- As head of council, assigning and sometimes joining protector missions
- Could trigger side quests with better loot

---

## Downtime (Weekends)

### Smoke Sessions with Tyren
- Nalyd and Tyren smoke between responsibilities and on rest days
- Could be a social mechanic: recover stress, swap info, hear rumors

---

## Character Notes
- Eats alone, spends a lot of time training solo
- Close friends: Tyren and Bonesy. Tolerates Nibby when he tags along.
- Earns money through council job and dojo
- Rests on weekends but still trains
- Djinn taking over the dojo full-time

# Aly

Based on Ash's character description.

## Daily Duties

### Rooftop Patrols / The Watcher
- Aly moves like a shadow across rooftops day and night, gathering intel
- Collects every rumor, every scrap of knowledge from hidden corners
- Uses **thaumaturgy** to conjure an illusion of her bow (Stormpiercer) to deter crime — never actually fires at petty criminals
- **Rewards:** Intel items, reputation with common folk, possible quest hooks from overheard info

### Hunting, Foraging & Harvesting
- Fishes rivers, forages woods, harvests wild bounty beyond the walls
- Occasionally joins Nalyd on giant squid hunts when he wants company
- **Rewards:** Food/materials for the community, small XP, relationship upkeep with Nalyd

### Anonymous Community Repairs
- Gathers timber and metal for repairs
- Mends shattered windows, straightens stones in the streets using magic
- Leaves offerings at doorsteps and shop counters after nightfall
- The people know it's her but she never claims it
- **Rewards:** Reputation with merchants/citizens, passive goodwill bonuses

### Magical Studies
- Studies magical tomes to sharpen her abilities
- Consumes a great deal of personal time
- **Rewards:** XP toward magical growth, chance at new spell/ability unlocks over time

---

## Ongoing Duties (No Fixed Schedule)

**Note:** Aly doesn't follow a weekly schedule. The spy life is unpredictable — she follows the intel where it leads.

### Council Meetings
- Attends as a founding member of The Collective
- Reports intel gathered from her network
- **Rewards:** Stipend gold, access to council decisions and missions

### Queen's Shadows Operations (Working Name)
- Coordinates her 5-woman covert spy network (Aly + 4 others, all different races)
- Off-the-books intel group that fills gaps where Tyren's Shield, Navy, and Rangers don't have consistent presence
- **Operating Areas:** Slums and wooded areas
- Medieval Charlie's Angels vibe (loosely)
- **Rewards:** High-value intel, covert mission triggers, unique items from the underground

### Dragon Rebirth Project
- Ongoing magical experiment: merging bone dust from her dragon-bone bow with her Spiritual Weapon
- Goal: replace the lifeless arrow with a living creature (Baby Groot style rebirth)
- **Status:** DM's call on progress and breakthrough timing
- **Rewards:** Major milestone — living companion, evolved spiritual weapon mechanic

### Appreciating the Arts
- Spends free time admiring creativity throughout Okhan
- Enjoys the people's art, music, performances
- Could tie into social encounters or discovery of hidden messages in artwork

---

## Character Notes
- Unofficial title: "The Watcher of Okhan"
- Moves unseen, acts anonymously, denies credit for her good deeds
- Core tenant: Sacrifice
- Bow (Stormpiercer) made from dragon bones
- Spiritual Weapon currently manifests as a lifeless arrow she can direct
- Spy group name not finalized (working title: "The Queen's Shadows")
- 4 other women in the spy network TBD

# Random Events

Ideas for unexpected events that can trigger during duties, patrols, or downtime. These add variety and unpredictability to daily life in Okhan.

---

## Combat / Threat Events

### Creature Incursion
- Unusual beast spotted near the walls or in the woods (not a squid)
- Could be a displaced predator, corrupted animal, or something nobody's seen before
- Scales with whoever encounters it

### Bar Brawl at the Dragon's Hollow
- Tensions boil over between patrons, maybe outsiders causing trouble
- Could involve The Shield responding, or a PC who happens to be there
- Social or combat resolution

### Smuggler Ambush
- The Shadows or Shield stumble on a smuggling operation in the slums or docks
- Armed resistance, chase through alleys, contraband to investigate
- Could tie into larger plot threads

### Arena Upset
- A gladiatorial challenger shows up with unusual abilities or a grudge
- Could target Nalyd specifically, or just be a wildcard fighter
- Chance for rare loot or a rival NPC introduction

### Coastal Raid Attempt
- Small hostile vessel approaches Okhan's waters
- Navy intercepts but could escalate if PCs get involved
- Prisoners to interrogate, cargo to inspect

---

## Social / Political Events

### Foreign Diplomat Arrival
- Envoy from a distant land requests audience with the Queen
- Could bring trade offers, warnings, or hidden agendas
- Council meeting triggered, intel gathering for the Shadows

### Citizen Dispute
- Two merchants, families, or guilds in a heated disagreement
- Needs mediation — could go to The Shield, the council, or a PC directly
- Reputation consequences based on how it's handled

### Festival Preparations
- A holiday, memorial, or celebration is coming up
- PCs contribute based on their roles (Tyren: security, Aly: intel sweep, Nalyd: arena event, etc.)
- Community bonding, possible sabotage attempt

### Political Rumor
- Whispers of dissent, corruption, or a power play within Okhan's leadership
- Aly's network picks it up first
- Investigation quest, could be true or a deliberate misdirection

### Mysterious Stranger
- An outsider arrives in Okhan with unclear intentions
- Stays at the tavern, asks odd questions, watches people
- Multiple PCs might notice independently — who investigates first?

---

## Discovery / Exploration Events

### Ancient Ruins Found
- Workers or patrols discover something buried or hidden near Okhan
- Old structure, sealed chamber, carved symbols nobody recognizes
- Exploration quest with puzzle/trap/lore elements

### Rare Tome Surfaces
- A magical text appears in a market stall, gifted by a traveler, or found in old storage
- Could advance Aly's studies or contain dangerous knowledge
- Authenticity might be questionable

### Strange Weather
- Unnatural fog, an unseasonal storm, or lights in the sky
- Magical origin? Natural anomaly? Omen?
- Investigation could reveal something deeper or just be atmosphere

### Hidden Cache
- Someone finds a stash of old weapons, coins, or documents from before The Collective's rule
- Could be harmless history or evidence of something buried on purpose
- Finders keepers vs. turning it in to the council

---

## Character-Specific Triggers

### Nalyd: Dojo Challenge
- A former student or wandering martial artist challenges the dojo's reputation
- Nalyd can handle it personally or let Djinn deal with it
- Pride and legacy on the line

### Tyren: Shield Misconduct
- Report that one of The Shield's officers overstepped or took a bribe
- Tests Tyren's justice philosophy — internal affairs investigation
- How he handles it sets precedent

### Aly: Shadow Compromised
- One of the Queen's Shadows might be followed, captured, or turned
- Aly has to extract, investigate, or cut ties
- Trust and loyalty tested

### Acacia: Crown Pressure
- External or internal pressure on the Queen to make a controversial decision
- The Collective weighs in, but ultimately it's her call
- Liberation vs. responsibility tension

### Nibby: Ghost Sighting
- Someone claims to have seen Nibby — which shouldn't happen if he's doing his job
- Is his cover slipping, or is someone impersonating him?
- Paranoia and investigation

---

## Economy / Resource Events

### Trade Caravan Arrives
- New goods, rare materials, exotic items available temporarily
- Prices fluctuate, shop inventory refreshes
- Could bring news from other regions

### Resource Shortage
- Timber, food, or metal supply dips due to weather, pests, or disrupted routes
- Community effort to address it, PCs contribute based on their skills
- Urgency scales with severity

### Black Market Tip
- The Shadows discover an underground market dealing in stolen or forbidden goods
- Raid it, infiltrate it, or monitor it for intel
- Rare items available at moral cost

### Grumm's Special Order
- The bear gets a shipment of something unusual
- Limited stock, first come first served
- Could be a crafting material, enchanted item, or just really good honey
