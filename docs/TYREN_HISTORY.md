# Tyren Henvar - Known History

## Overview

Commander Tyren Henvar, known as "Stone Mane," is one of the legendary heroes who defeated King Sythrall. He now runs Okhan's military forces: The Okhan Shield (police), the Army Ranger Corps, and the Royal Naval Fleet.

**This is public knowledge NPCs would reasonably know or have heard about.**

---

## The Ashen Vow (Tyren's Past)

A legendary brotherhood of rangers founded by **Thalen Varic**. They stood for justice, growth, and true brotherhood. Tyren joined as a teenager after Thalen found him half-dead and saw potential in him.

- **Thalen Varic** — The First Ranger. Founder of The Ashen Vow. Tyren's mentor and father figure. Taught Tyren everything about being a ranger.
- **Hunter Drayen** — Tyren's assigned partner in the Vow. Bulkier, more physical, far less thoughtful. Believed in swift endings over justice.

### The Break

During a mission to apprehend a corrupt noble, Tyren pleaded for a fair trial. Hunter ignored him and executed the man in cold blood. That was the last time they stood side by side. Tyren and Hunter parted ways permanently.

---

## The Iron Howl Legion

After Tyren left, **Hunter Drayen rose through the Ashen Vow's ranks and corrupted it from within.** He twisted the brotherhood into a brutal military force called the **Iron Howl Legion**.

- Hunter crowned himself **"The Steel Wolf"**
- He corrupted Tyren's lion symbol into a "lion made of fangs" — a mockery
- **Thalen Varic was killed during Hunter's coup**
- The Legion became a feared force, terrorizing the land

### The Final Note

Before his death, Thalen sent Tyren his broken badge and a final message:

> *"Stone-Mane— The Vow has fallen. Hunter wears a lion made of fangs. Seek the Verdict in the frost below. Remember who you are. —T.V."*

Tyren received this during the Sythrall Campaign.

---

## Hunter's Death

**Hunter Drayen was killed in the Raven-Well beneath Ravenguard** during the final chapter of the Sythrall Campaign. 

Before the end, Sythrall desecrated Hunter's corpse, reanimating it as a Bone Amalgamation. But before the transformation completed, Hunter's eyes opened one last time. He looked at Tyren with profound regret. Then he was gone.

---

## The Hunt (Post-War)

With Hunter dead, Tyren embarked on a personal quest to **destroy every remaining Iron Howl Legion force**. He hunted them across the land.

The final battle was saving the **Calrissian Tribe** — an ancient tribe of exotic bird trainers — from the last of the Legion.

**The Iron Howl Legion is 100% destroyed.** Wiped from existence by Tyren's hand.

The tribe gifted Tyren his falcon, **Cal (Calarion Calrissian)**, in gratitude.

## How NPCs Should Reference This

### Appropriate:
- "The Iron Howl Legion? Tyren destroyed them years ago. Every last one."
- "Stone Mane doesn't talk about Hunter. Don't bring it up."
- "Thalen Varic was like a father to him. The Steel Wolf killed him."
- "The Ashen Vow was everything Tyren believed in. Then Hunter twisted it."

### Avoid:
- Making up new Iron Howl survivors (they're ALL dead)
- Treating Hunter's death as anything but final
- Inventing details about Tyren's childhood/family (that's private)
- Bringing up Thalen casually — it's a deep wound

---

## Important Notes

- **The Iron Howl Legion is GONE.** No remnants. No secret survivors. Tyren made sure of that.
- **Hunter Drayen is DEAD.** Killed in the Raven-Well, desecrated by Sythrall.
- **This is Tyren's heaviest history.** NPCs should treat it with weight, not casual gossip.
- **The 17th** is significant to Tyren (the day he joined the Vow). He does major things on the 17th.
