# The Blackthorn Clan

## Overview

The Blackthorn Clan is a criminal syndicate operating in the shadows of Okhan. They're the kingdom's open secret — everyone knows they exist, no one can prove anything, and their influence creeps into every corner of the city's underbelly.

**Use this as the default antagonist for player-driven RP when stakes are needed.**

---

## What They Are

- **Smugglers first, criminals second.** Their core operation is moving goods through unofficial channels — contraband, stolen merchandise, and things people don't want tracked.
- **Slum-based.** They operate out of Okhan's poorest districts, where the Shield's patrols are thin and the people are desperate enough to look away.
- **Connected.** They have contacts in merchant guilds, dock workers, and possibly even minor city officials. Nothing confirmed, but the whispers are there.

## Known Operations

- **Northern smuggling route:** Abandoned docks and old forest roads. A merchant dealing in "silks" is suspected as their front.
- **Protection rackets:** Small businesses in the slums pay them to avoid "accidents."
- **Fencing:** Stolen goods from across the kingdom end up moving through their network.
- **Information brokering:** They know things. They sell things. Secrets have value.

## Leadership

**Unknown.** The Blackthorn Clan doesn't have a public face. Rumors mention:
- "The Thornlord" — probably a title, not a name
- A council of senior members called "The Root"
- Someone on the inside of Okhan's legitimate power structure

*Feel free to invent lieutenants, enforcers, or contacts as needed for RP. Nothing is canon until it happens in play.*

---

## How NPCs Should Use This

### When to invoke Blackthorn:
- Player wants to investigate something shady → Blackthorn connection
- Mysterious figures spotted → could be Blackthorn scouts
- Something stolen or smuggled → their network
- Tension in the slums → their influence
- Need a "someone's behind this" → Blackthorn

### When NOT to invoke Blackthorn:
- Player wants a self-contained story (respect that)
- It would overshadow what the player is actually trying to do
- The situation is clearly supernatural/magical (that's different territory)
- It would conflict with established campaign lore

### Tone:
- They're **dangerous but not apocalyptic.** Crime syndicate, not world-ending threat.
- They're **smart.** They don't leave evidence. They don't make dramatic speeches. They just... operate.
- They're **patient.** They've been here for years. They'll be here for years more.

## Story Hooks (Use Freely)

- A merchant is found dead in the slums. No witnesses. Clean job.
- Stolen military supplies showing up on the black market.
- Someone's paying good gold for information about Shield patrol routes.
- A new "business" opened in the slums. Very polite. Very insistent about "community contributions."
- Rangers found marked trees along the northern route. Blackthorn signals?
- A Veil operative went dark in the slums. Last known contact: investigating Blackthorn.

## Important: Avoid "The Rot"

The Rot / fungal corruption / Bound By Rot campaign content is **OFF LIMITS** for NPC roleplay. That's Blake's actual D&D campaign. Keep Blackthorn Clan completely separate — they're mundane criminals, not connected to any supernatural plague or corruption.
